# 3. About the Policy

**RSBN Mediclaim Suraksha (RSBN/MCS/2026)** is a traditional, affordably-priced indemnity health insurance policy designed to provide reliable hospitalisation coverage to individuals and families through a wide network of empanelled hospitals, including Government and municipal hospitals, across India.

The plan follows a conventional, well-established mediclaim structure that many policyholders in India are familiar with:

- Defined sub-limits on room rent and ICU charges as a percentage of Sum Insured, keeping premiums affordable.
- Disease-wise and procedure-wise capping on select common ailments.
- A wide, pan-India network of empanelled hospitals, including major Government hospitals.
- Straightforward Cumulative Bonus structure that rewards claim-free years.

This Policy Document, the Policy Schedule, the Customer Information Sheet, the Proposal Form, and any endorsements together form the complete contract between the Policyholder and Rashtriya Swasthya Bima Nigam Ltd.

# 8. Benefits

### 8.1 In-Patient Hospitalisation Cover
Covers room, boarding, and nursing expenses (subject to the room rent sub-limit), ICU charges, surgeon's and doctor's fees, anaesthesia, blood, oxygen, operation theatre charges, medicines, and diagnostic tests for hospitalisation exceeding 24 hours, up to the Sum Insured (plus accrued Cumulative Bonus).

### 8.2 Pre-Hospitalisation & Post-Hospitalisation Cover
- **Pre-Hospitalisation:** Medical expenses incurred up to 30 days prior to admission, related to the same illness/injury.
- **Post-Hospitalisation:** Medical expenses incurred up to 60 days after discharge, related to the same illness/injury.

### 8.3 Day Care Procedures
Covers a defined list of day-care procedures not requiring 24-hour hospitalisation, as specified in the Annexure to the Policy Schedule.

### 8.4 Cumulative Bonus
For every claim-free Policy Year, the Sum Insured increases by 5%, up to a maximum of 50% of the base Sum Insured, at no additional premium. Upon a claim being made, the Cumulative Bonus reduces by 5% at the next renewal, though the base Sum Insured is never reduced below the amount originally opted for.

### 8.5 Disease-wise / Procedure-wise Sub-limits
Select common ailments and procedures (e.g., cataract, hernia, hydrocele, piles, sinusitis, knee replacement) are subject to sub-limits expressed as a percentage of Sum Insured or a fixed monetary cap, as detailed in the Annexure to the Policy Schedule. Charges beyond the specified sub-limit are payable by the Insured Person.

### 8.6 AYUSH Treatment Cover
In-patient treatment under Ayurveda, Yoga & Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, and Homeopathy (AYUSH) systems, taken at a registered Government or Government-recognised AYUSH hospital, is covered up to 25% of Sum Insured, subject to a maximum of ₹50,000 per Policy Year.

### 8.7 Ambulance Cover
Road ambulance expenses are covered up to ₹1,500 per hospitalisation event.

### 8.8 Optional Covers / Add-ons

| Add-on | Description |
|---|---|
| Personal Accident Cover | Additional lump sum benefit on accidental death/disability of the Insured Person |
| Room Rent Waiver | Removes the standard room rent sub-limit, subject to additional premium |
| Critical Illness Cover | Lump sum payout on diagnosis of any of 10 listed critical illnesses |
| Domiciliary Hospitalisation | Covers treatment taken at home for a condition that would otherwise require hospitalisation, where hospital beds are unavailable or the patient's condition does not permit transportation |

# 10. Exclusions

### 10.1 Permanent Exclusions
- Cosmetic or plastic surgery, unless required due to accident or medically necessary reconstruction.
- Treatment for obesity/weight control, unless medically necessary and pre-authorised.
- Dental treatment or surgery, unless requiring hospitalisation due to accident.

- Investigation and treatment for infertility, unless covered under a specific opted add-on.
- Expenses arising from self-inflicted injury, attempted suicide, or participation in a criminal act.
- War, invasion, act of foreign enemy, hostilities, civil war, nuclear, biological, or chemical contamination.

### 10.2 Standard Exclusions (Time-bound / Conditional)
- Pre-existing diseases, until completion of the applicable PED waiting period.
- Specific listed illnesses/procedures, until completion of the applicable waiting period.
- Non-allopathic treatment not undertaken at a registered AYUSH hospital.
- Charges for a room category exceeding entitlement, resulting in proportionate deduction of associated medical expenses (room, ICU, doctor's fees, and other charges directly linked to room category) in the same proportion as the excess room rent bears to the actual room rent charged.
- Hospitalisation not medically necessary or where treatment could reasonably be provided on an out-patient basis.
- Expenses incurred outside India.

# 11. Claim Process

### 11.1 Cashless Claim Process (Network Hospital)

1. Identify an RSBN-empanelled network hospital (list available at branches, on the website, and via helpline).
2. Produce the RSBN Mediclaim Card and photo ID at the hospital's insurance/TPA desk.
3. The hospital submits the Pre-Authorisation Request Form to RSBN's Third-Party Administrator (TPA) or in-house Claims Team.
4. RSBN/TPA reviews and communicates the pre-authorisation decision, typically within 1 hour of receipt of complete information, in line with IRDAI cashless claim turnaround guidelines.
5. Upon discharge, the hospital submits the final bill for cashless settlement directly with RSBN/TPA, subject to policy terms, room rent sub-limits, and applicable co-payment.

### 11.2 Reimbursement Claim Process (Non-Network Hospital)

1. Intimate RSBN within 24 hours of emergency admission (or prior to admission for planned hospitalisation).
2. Pay hospital bills directly and collect all original bills, reports, and discharge summary.
3. Submit the duly filled Claim Form (available at branches or on the website) along with all original documents within 15 days of discharge, at the nearest RSBN branch office or through post.
4. RSBN's Claims Team/TPA reviews the documents and may seek clarification or additional documents, if required.
5. On approval, the claim amount (after applicable sub-limits and co-payment deductions) is reimbursed directly to the Policyholder's registered bank account.

### 11.3 Claim Settlement Timelines
- Cashless pre-authorisation decision: within 1 hour of receipt of complete information.
- Final cashless bill settlement after discharge: within 3 hours of receipt of complete discharge documents, wherever feasible.
- Reimbursement claims: settled within 15 days of receipt of all required documents, as per applicable regulatory timelines.

# 13. Premium Payment Rules

### 13.1 Grace Period
A Grace Period of **30 days** is allowed for renewal after the Policy expiry date, during which continuity benefits (waiting period credit, Cumulative Bonus, moratorium period) are preserved, though coverage remains suspended until renewal premium is paid.

### 13.2 Renewal
The Policy is renewable annually, for life, subject to timely payment of premium. RSBN shall not deny renewal on the sole ground of age or past claims, except on grounds of established fraud or moral hazard, in accordance with IRDAI guidelines.

### 13.3 Free Look Period
The Policyholder may review this Policy within **30 days** of receipt of the Policy Document and may cancel it if not agreeable to the terms, provided no claim has been made, subject to deduction of proportionate risk premium for the period on cover and any expenses already incurred by the Company.

### 13.4 Cancellation & Refund
- The Policyholder may cancel the Policy at any time with written notice, and shall be entitled to a refund of premium for the unexpired period on a short-period scale, provided no claim has been made during the Policy Year.
- RSBN may cancel the Policy on grounds of established fraud or non-disclosure, subject to 15 days' prior written notice and applicable law.

### 13.5 Portability
Policyholders may port this Policy to another insurer, or migrate to another RSBN product, at renewal, subject to IRDAI portability guidelines, carrying forward continuity benefits up to the Sum Insured of the previous policy.

# 15. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1. What is RSBN Mediclaim Suraksha?**
It is a traditional, affordably-priced indemnity health insurance policy providing hospitalisation cover through a wide network of empanelled hospitals, including major Government hospitals.

**Q2. Why does this plan have a room rent limit?**
The room rent sub-limit (1% of Sum Insured per day for a normal ward, 2% for ICU) is a standard cost-control feature that helps keep premiums more affordable compared to zero-capping plans.

**Q3. What happens if I choose a room above my eligible category?**
A proportionate deduction will apply to room charges and associated medical expenses (doctor's fees, nursing, etc.), in proportion to the excess over the eligible room rent limit.

**Q4. Is co-payment applicable on this policy?**
Yes, a 10% co-payment applies to all claims where the Insured Person's entry age is 61 years or above, and a 20% co-payment applies for treatment taken at a non-network hospital.

**Q5. What is the Cumulative Bonus?**
For every claim-free Policy Year, the Sum Insured increases by 5%, up to a maximum of 50%, at no additional premium.

**Q6. Are there sub-limits on specific diseases?**
Yes, select common procedures (e.g., cataract, hernia, knee replacement) carry disease-wise sub-limits as detailed in the Annexure to the Policy Schedule.

**Q7. What is the waiting period for pre-existing diseases?**
36 months of continuous coverage from the date of first inception of the policy with RSBN, as per applicable IRDAI norms.

**Q8. Can I choose a multi-year policy term?**
No, this Policy is issued on an annual basis only and must be renewed each year.

**Q9. How do I avail cashless treatment?**
Visit any RSBN-empanelled network hospital, present your Mediclaim Card and photo ID, and the hospital will coordinate pre-authorisation with RSBN or its TPA.

**Q10. What is the turnaround time for cashless approval?**
As per IRDAI guidelines, cashless pre-authorisation decisions are typically communicated within 1 hour of receipt of complete information from the hospital.

**Q11. Can I get treatment at a non-network hospital?**
Yes, through the reimbursement route, though a 20% co-payment applies for treatment at non-network hospitals.

**Q12. What is the moratorium period?**
After 5 years of continuous coverage, RSBN cannot reject a claim on grounds of non-disclosure or misrepresentation, except in cases of proven fraud, as per IRDAI regulations.

**Q13. Is there a maximum entry age for this policy?**
No, there is no maximum entry age, in line with IRDAI's lifetime renewability guidelines.

**Q14. What happens if I miss my renewal date?**
A Grace Period of 30 days is provided. Coverage remains suspended during this period; continuity benefits are preserved only if renewed within the Grace Period.

**Q15. Can I remove the room rent capping?**
Yes, through the optional Room Rent Waiver add-on, available at an additional premium.

**Q16. Is domiciliary hospitalisation covered?**
Only under the optional Domiciliary Hospitalisation add-on, for conditions that would otherwise require hospitalisation but treatment is taken at home due to non-availability of a hospital bed or the patient's condition not permitting transfer.

**Q17. What is AYUSH cover under this policy?**
In-patient AYUSH treatment at a Government or Government-recognised AYUSH hospital is covered up to 25% of Sum Insured, subject to a maximum of ₹50,000 per Policy Year.

**Q18. What is not covered under this Policy?**
Cosmetic treatments, dental treatment (unless due to accident), self-inflicted injuries, and treatment outside India are among the key exclusions. Refer to Section 10 for the complete list.

**Q19. How can I check my claim status?**
By contacting the nearest RSBN branch office or the toll-free helpline with your claim reference number.

**Q20. Is a free health check-up available under this plan?**
No, this Policy does not include a complimentary annual health check-up as a standard base-plan benefit.

**Q21. What tax benefit can I claim on my premium?**
Premiums qualify for deduction under Section 80D of the Income Tax Act, 1961, subject to prescribed limits based on the age of the insured members.

**Q22. Can I port my existing health policy to RSBN?**
Yes, subject to IRDAI portability guidelines, at the time of renewal of your existing policy.
