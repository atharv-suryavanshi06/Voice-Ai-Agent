# 3. About the Policy

**TrustShield Health Suraksha (TS/HSK/2026)** is a comprehensive, indemnity-based health insurance plan offering high Sum Insured options, unlimited automatic restoration, and global emergency cover, designed for individuals and families seeking extensive protection against escalating healthcare costs.

The plan is built to address three core needs of a modern policyholder:

- **Adequate cover:** High Sum Insured options up to ₹3 Crore, suitable for tier-1 city healthcare costs.
- **Unlimited protection within the year:** Automatic, unlimited restoration of Sum Insured for unrelated illnesses, and once for related illnesses, within the same Policy Year.
- **Global emergency support:** Emergency hospitalisation cover outside India for listed critical illnesses, under the Global variant.

This Policy Document, the Policy Schedule, the Customer Information Sheet, the Proposal Form, and any endorsements together form the complete contract between the Policyholder and TrustShield General Insurance Co. Ltd.

# 8. Benefits

### 8.1 In-Patient Hospitalisation Cover
Covers room rent (no capping under Secure/Global), boarding, nursing, ICU/ICCU charges, surgeon's and anaesthetist's fees, operation theatre charges, medicines, implants, and diagnostic tests for hospitalisation exceeding 24 hours.

### 8.2 Pre-Hospitalisation & Post-Hospitalisation Cover
- **Pre-Hospitalisation:** Medical expenses incurred up to 60 days prior to admission, related to the same illness/injury.
- **Post-Hospitalisation:** Medical expenses incurred up to 180 days after discharge, related to the same illness/injury.

### 8.3 Day Care Procedures
Covers all medical/surgical procedures that do not require 24-hour hospitalisation due to technological advancement, without restriction to a specific list, subject to medical necessity.

### 8.4 Restoration Benefit
The Sum Insured is automatically restored by 100% on exhaustion, an unlimited number of times in a Policy Year, for treatment of an unrelated illness/injury of any Insured Person. For the same illness/injury for which a claim has already been paid, restoration is available once per Policy Year.

### 8.5 Multiplier Benefit
The Sum Insured increases by 50% of the base Sum Insured for the first claim-free year, and by a further 25% for the second consecutive claim-free year, up to a maximum of 100% cumulative increase, at no additional premium. In case of a claim, the accrued Multiplier Benefit reduces proportionately, subject to the base Sum Insured always being protected.

### 8.6 Air Ambulance Cover
Emergency air ambulance transportation to the nearest appropriate hospital is covered up to ₹5,00,000 per Policy Year, under the Secure and Global variants.

### 8.7 Global Emergency Cover (Optional – Global Variant)
Covers emergency in-patient hospitalisation expenses incurred outside India for a listed set of critical illnesses (e.g., cancer, heart attack, major organ transplant), up to the Sum Insured or a specified sub-limit, subject to prior intimation and pre-authorisation requirements.

### 8.8 Consumables Cover (Optional Add-on)
Covers cost of consumables (gloves, PPE kits, syringes, and similar items) typically excluded under standard hospitalisation cover, up to the Sum Insured, when opted for as an add-on.

### 8.9 Annual Health Check-up
A comprehensive annual health check-up is offered to all Insured Persons, regardless of whether a claim was made in the preceding Policy Year, through TrustShield's network diagnostic partners.

### 8.10 AYUSH Treatment Cover
In-patient AYUSH treatment (Ayurveda, Yoga & Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, Homeopathy) at a registered AYUSH hospital is covered up to the Sum Insured.

### 8.11 Optional Covers / Add-ons

| Add-on | Description |
|---|---|
| Consumables Cover | Covers non-medical consumables typically excluded from standard hospitalisation claims |
| Air Ambulance Enhancement | Increases the air ambulance sub-limit beyond the standard ₹5,00,000 |
| Global Emergency Cover | Extends cover to emergency hospitalisation for critical illnesses outside India |
| OPD & Wellness Cover | Reimbursement of outpatient consultations, diagnostics, and pharmacy expenses |
| Critical Illness Booster | Additional lump sum benefit on first diagnosis of a listed critical illness |

# 10. Exclusions

### 10.1 Permanent Exclusions
- Cosmetic or plastic surgery, unless necessitated by an accident or medically required reconstruction.
- Treatment for obesity/weight control surgery, unless medically necessary and pre-authorised (bariatric surgery covered only where BMI and clinical criteria are met, as per policy wording).

- Expenses arising from self-inflicted injury, attempted suicide, or participation in an unlawful act.
- War, invasion, act of foreign enemy, hostilities, civil war, nuclear, biological, or chemical contamination.
- Unproven, experimental, or investigational treatment not recognised by prevailing medical practice.

### 10.2 Standard Exclusions (Time-bound / Conditional)
- Pre-existing diseases, until completion of the applicable PED waiting period.
- Specific listed illnesses/procedures, until completion of the applicable waiting period.

- Expenses incurred outside India, unless the Global Emergency Cover add-on has been opted for and the claim meets the specified critical illness criteria.
- Consumables and non-medical items, unless the Consumables Cover add-on has been opted for.

# 11. Claim Process

### 11.1 Cashless Claim Process (Network Hospital)

1. Locate a TrustShield network hospital via the website, app, or helpline.
2. Present the TrustShield Health Card and a valid photo ID at the hospital's TPA/insurance desk.
3. The hospital submits a Pre-Authorisation Request electronically to TrustShield.
4. TrustShield reviews and communicates its decision, typically within 1 hour of receipt of complete information, in line with IRDAI cashless claim turnaround guidelines.
5. On discharge, the hospital submits the final bill directly to TrustShield for settlement, subject to policy terms and applicable co-payment/deductible, if any.

### 11.2 Reimbursement Claim Process (Non-Network Hospital)

1. Intimate TrustShield within 24 hours of emergency admission (or prior to admission for planned hospitalisation).
2. Settle hospital bills directly and retain all original bills, reports, and the discharge summary.
3. Submit the completed Claim Form with all supporting original documents within 15 days of discharge.
4. TrustShield's Claims Team reviews the submission and may request additional clarification or documents.
5. On approval, the eligible claim amount is credited directly to the Policyholder's registered bank account.

### 11.3 Claim Settlement Timelines
- Cashless pre-authorisation decision: within 1 hour of receipt of complete information.
- Final cashless bill settlement: within 3 hours of receipt of complete discharge documents, wherever feasible.
- Reimbursement claims: settled within 15 days of receipt of all required documents, as per applicable regulatory timelines.

# 13. Premium Payment Rules

### 13.1 Grace Period
A Grace Period of **30 days** is allowed for renewal after the Policy expiry date. Continuity benefits (waiting periods served, Multiplier Benefit, moratorium period) are preserved if renewed within this period, though coverage remains suspended until renewal premium is paid.

### 13.2 Renewal
The Policy is renewable for life, subject to timely payment of premium. TrustShield shall not deny renewal on the sole ground of age or past claims, except on grounds of established fraud or moral hazard, in accordance with IRDAI guidelines.

### 13.3 Free Look Period
The Policyholder may review this Policy within **30 days** of receipt of the Policy Document and may cancel it if not agreeable to the terms, provided no claim has been made, subject to deduction of proportionate risk premium for the period on cover and any expenses already incurred by the Company.

### 13.4 Cancellation & Refund
- The Policyholder may cancel the Policy at any time with written notice, and shall be entitled to a refund of premium for the unexpired period on a short-period scale, provided no claim has been made during the Policy Year.
- TrustShield may cancel the Policy on grounds of established fraud or non-disclosure, subject to 15 days' prior written notice and applicable law.

### 13.5 Portability
Policyholders may port this Policy to another insurer, or migrate to another TrustShield product, at renewal, subject to IRDAI portability guidelines, carrying forward continuity benefits up to the Sum Insured of the previous policy.

# 15. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1. What is TrustShield Health Suraksha?**
It is a comprehensive, indemnity-based health insurance plan offering high Sum Insured options, unlimited restoration for unrelated illnesses, and optional global emergency cover.

**Q2. What is the Multiplier Benefit?**
It is a benefit under which the Sum Insured increases by 50% after the first claim-free year and a further 25% after the second, up to a maximum of 100% cumulative increase, without additional premium.

**Q3. How is the Multiplier Benefit different from a standard No Claim Bonus?**
The Multiplier Benefit accrues at a higher rate (50% in year one) compared to typical cumulative bonus structures, allowing the effective cover to grow faster for claim-free policyholders.

**Q4. Is there a cap on room rent?**
No, there is no capping on room rent under the Secure and Global variants; the Classic variant follows a Single Private Room limit.

**Q5. What is covered under the Restoration Benefit?**
The Sum Insured is restored 100%, unlimited times, for unrelated illnesses/injuries in the same Policy Year. For the same illness, restoration is available once per year.

**Q6. Does this plan cover treatment outside India?**
Only under the Global variant, with the optional Global Emergency Cover add-on, for a defined list of critical illnesses requiring emergency hospitalisation.

**Q7. What is the waiting period for pre-existing diseases?**
36 months of continuous coverage from the date of first inception of the policy with TrustShield, as per applicable IRDAI norms.

**Q8. How do I use the cashless facility?**
Visit a TrustShield network hospital, present your Health Card and photo ID, and the hospital's insurance desk will coordinate pre-authorisation with TrustShield.

**Q9. What is the turnaround time for cashless approval?**
As per IRDAI norms, cashless pre-authorisation decisions are typically communicated within 1 hour of receipt of complete information from the hospital.

**Q10. Can I claim for treatment at a non-network hospital?**
Yes, through the reimbursement process; pay the hospital directly and submit the required documents to TrustShield within 15 days of discharge.

**Q11. What is the moratorium period?**
After 5 years of continuous coverage, TrustShield cannot reject a claim on grounds of non-disclosure or misrepresentation, except where fraud is established, as per IRDAI regulations.

**Q12. Is there a maximum entry age for this policy?**
No, there is no maximum entry age, in line with IRDAI's lifetime renewability guidelines.

**Q13. What happens if I do not renew my policy on time?**
A Grace Period of 30 days is provided. Coverage remains suspended during this period; continuity benefits are preserved only if renewal occurs within the Grace Period.

**Q14. Can I switch from another insurer to TrustShield?**
Yes, through portability at the time of renewal of your existing policy, subject to IRDAI portability guidelines and TrustShield's underwriting policy.

**Q15. Is air ambulance covered?**
Yes, up to ₹5,00,000 per Policy Year under the Secure and Global variants; the limit can be enhanced through an optional add-on.

**Q16. Are pre and post hospitalisation expenses covered?**
Yes, 60 days before admission and 180 days after discharge, for expenses related to the same illness/injury.

**Q17. Is maternity covered under the base plan?**
No, maternity is available only through an optional add-on, subject to a 36-month waiting period and applicable sub-limits.

**Q18. What is excluded under this Policy?**
Cosmetic treatments, dental treatment (unless due to accident), self-inflicted injury, and non-consumable-covered items are among key exclusions. Refer to Section 10 for the full list.

**Q19. How can I track my claim status?**
Through the TrustShield mobile app, customer web portal, or by contacting the 24x7 toll-free helpline with your claim reference number.

**Q20. Is an annual health check-up included?**
Yes, all Insured Persons are entitled to an annual health check-up, irrespective of claims made in the previous Policy Year.

**Q21. What tax benefit is available on my premium?**
Premiums qualify for deduction under Section 80D of the Income Tax Act, 1961, subject to prescribed limits based on the age of the insured members.

**Q22. Can I enhance my Sum Insured at renewal?**
Yes, subject to underwriting approval; a fresh waiting period applies only to the incremental Sum Insured amount.

**Q23. What happens to the Multiplier Benefit if I make a claim?**
The accrued Multiplier Benefit reduces proportionately in the year following a claim, though the base Sum Insured remains protected at the originally opted level.

**Q24. Does this plan cover consumables like gloves and PPE kits?**
Only if the optional Consumables Cover add-on has been opted for; otherwise, such items are excluded under the base plan.
