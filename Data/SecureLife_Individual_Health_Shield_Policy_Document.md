## 1. About the Policy

SecureLife Individual Health Shield is an individual, indemnity-based health insurance plan underwritten by SecureLife Insurance Pvt. Ltd. The policy is designed for individuals who wish to secure standalone hospitalization coverage along with an in-built Critical Illness benefit, Hospital Cash benefit, and Accidental Hospitalization cover.

The policy indemnifies the insured person for Reasonable and Customary medical expenses incurred towards hospitalization for an Illness or Injury, subject to the Sum Insured, waiting periods, sub-limits, and exclusions specified in this document. This plan is issued on a single-life basis; each insured individual is covered under a separate policy with an independent Sum Insured.

This document forms part of the policy contract and should be read together with the Policy Schedule, Proposal Form, and any endorsements issued by the Company.

## 6. Benefits

### 6.1 Hospitalization Benefit (Base Cover)
Covers Room Rent, Nursing Charges, ICU Charges, Doctor's Fees, Surgeon's Fees, Anaesthesia, Blood, Oxygen, OT Charges, Medicines, and Investigations for In-patient treatment exceeding 24 hours, up to the Sum Insured.

### 6.2 Pre and Post-Hospitalization Benefit
- Pre-hospitalization medical expenses covered for 30 days immediately before admission.
- Post-hospitalization medical expenses covered for 60 days immediately after discharge.

### 6.3 Day Care Treatment
All medical/surgical procedures requiring less than 24 hours of hospitalization due to technological advancement (e.g., cataract surgery, dialysis, chemotherapy) are covered, as per the Company's list of 150+ Day Care Procedures.

### 6.4 Critical Illness Benefit (In-built, Lump Sum, Payable Once)
On first diagnosis of any one of the following 16 listed Critical Illnesses, occurring after the waiting period and surviving the Survival Period of 30 days, a lump sum equal to 50% of the Base Sum Insured (subject to a maximum of ₹10,00,000) is payable in addition to the hospitalization claim:

| # | Critical Illness Covered |
|---|---|
| 1 | Cancer of Specified Severity |
| 2 | First Heart Attack of Specified Severity |
| 3 | Open Chest CABG |
| 4 | Open Heart Replacement/Repair of Heart Valves |
| 5 | Kidney Failure Requiring Regular Dialysis |
| 6 | Stroke Resulting in Permanent Symptoms |
| 7 | Major Organ / Bone Marrow Transplant |
| 8 | Permanent Paralysis of Limbs |
| 9 | Multiple Sclerosis with Persisting Symptoms |
| 10 | Motor Neuron Disease with Permanent Symptoms |
| 11 | Coma of Specified Severity |
| 12 | Third Degree Burns |
| 13 | Aorta Graft Surgery |
| 14 | End-stage Lung Disease |
| 15 | End-stage Liver Disease |
| 16 | Benign Brain Tumour |

*This benefit is payable only once during the lifetime of the policy. Upon payment, this rider terminates but the base hospitalization cover continues.*

### 6.5 Accidental Hospitalization Benefit
An additional 25% of the Base Sum Insured is available exclusively for hospitalization arising out of an Accident, over and above the base Sum Insured, subject to a maximum of ₹5,00,000 per policy year.

### 6.6 Premium Waiver Benefit
Upon the insured being diagnosed with any Critical Illness listed in Section 6.4 and a valid claim being admitted, the Company waives the premium due at the next renewal, subject to a maximum of one waiver during the lifetime of the policy.

### 6.7 Hospital Cash Benefit
A fixed cash benefit of ₹1,500 per day is payable for each completed 24-hour period of hospitalization, from the 2nd day onward, up to a maximum of 15 days per policy year. This is payable irrespective of actual expenses incurred and is intended to offset incidental, non-medical costs.

### 6.8 No Claim Bonus (Cumulative Bonus)
For every claim-free policy year, the Sum Insured increases by 10%, up to a maximum accumulation of 50% of the Base Sum Insured, at no additional premium.

### 6.9 Restoration Benefit
If the Sum Insured (including Cumulative Bonus) is exhausted in a policy year, 100% of the Base Sum Insured is automatically restored for that policy year, for claims unrelated to the illness/injury for which the original claim was made. Available once per policy year.

### 6.10 Optional Riders (Available on Payment of Additional Premium)

## 7. Exclusions

The following are **not covered** under this policy, unless specifically stated otherwise:

- Pre-existing Diseases, until 36 months of continuous coverage have elapsed (Waiting Period)
- Specific illnesses (e.g., cataract, hernia, hysterectomy, joint replacement) for the first 24 months of the policy (Specific Waiting Period)
- Any claim within the first 30 days of policy inception, except for Accidental Hospitalization
- Pregnancy, childbirth (including caesarean section), and related complications
- Cosmetic or plastic surgery, unless necessitated by an Accident
- Dental treatment or surgery, unless requiring hospitalization due to an Accident
- Treatment for obesity, weight control programs, and bariatric surgery, unless medically necessitated and pre-approved
- Self-inflicted injury, attempted suicide, or intentional self-harm
- Injury or illness arising from use of alcohol, drugs, or intoxicating substances
- Participation in adventure sports, professional sports, or hazardous activities
- War, invasion, act of foreign enemy, civil war, nuclear, chemical, or biological attack
- Treatment taken outside India
- Experimental, unproven, or investigational treatment not recognized by the medical council
- Any expenses related to donor screening or organ harvesting for a transplant where the insured is the donor
- Vitamins, tonics, and general debility treatment not related to a diagnosed illness
- Expenses on spectacles, contact lenses, hearing aids, and durable medical equipment unless specified in a rider

## 8. Claim Process

### 8.1 Cashless Claim Process
1. Identify a Network Hospital for planned or emergency treatment.
2. Present the Health Card and photo ID at the hospital's insurance desk.
3. Hospital submits a pre-authorization request to the Company/TPA.
4. Company/TPA reviews and issues approval (or query) typically within 4 hours for emergency cases and 48 hours before admission for planned cases.
5. On discharge, the hospital settles the bill directly with the Company/TPA, subject to policy terms and any non-payable items.

### 8.2 Reimbursement Claim Process
1. Intimate the claim to the Company within 48 hours of emergency admission, or 48 hours prior for a planned admission.
2. Undergo treatment and pay the hospital bills directly.
3. Submit the duly filled claim form along with original bills, reports, and discharge summary within 30 days of discharge.
4. Company/TPA reviews documents; may request additional information within 7 working days of receipt.
5. Claim is settled within 15 days of receipt of last necessary document, as per applicable regulatory timelines.

### 8.3 Critical Illness Claim Process
1. Intimate the diagnosis to the Company within 30 days of the diagnosis.
2. Submit the Critical Illness claim form along with diagnostic/histopathology reports confirming the specified severity.
3. Survive the applicable Survival Period of 30 days from the date of diagnosis.
4. Company reviews and disburses the lump sum benefit upon acceptance of the claim.

## 13. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1. What is the difference between cashless and reimbursement claims?**
In a cashless claim, the Company/TPA settles the bill directly with the network hospital. In a reimbursement claim, the insured pays the hospital first and later claims reimbursement from the Company.

**Q2. Can I choose any hospital for cashless treatment?**
Cashless facility is available only at hospitals within the Company's Network Hospital list. Treatment at a non-network hospital can still be claimed on a reimbursement basis.

**Q3. What happens if I don't renew my policy on time?**
You get a grace period of 30 days (15 days for monthly mode). If not renewed within this period, the policy lapses and all continuity benefits, including waiting period credit, are lost.

**Q4. Is Cancer covered from day one?**
No. Cancer, being a pre-existing or specific illness in certain contexts, is subject to the applicable waiting periods described in Section 7. If diagnosed after the waiting period is served, both hospitalization cover and the Critical Illness Benefit apply.

**Q5. Can I port this policy from another insurer?**
Yes, subject to IRDAI Portability Guidelines, credit for waiting periods already served with the previous insurer is given, provided the port request is made 45–60 days before the previous policy's renewal date.

**Q6. Does this plan cover maternity expenses?**
No. This individual plan does not cover pregnancy, childbirth, or related complications. Policyholders seeking maternity cover may consider the SecureLife Family Health Suraksha plan.

**Q7. What is Restoration Benefit and when does it apply?**
If your Sum Insured is fully utilized during the policy year, 100% of the Base Sum Insured is restored automatically, once per year, for future unrelated claims.

**Q8. Is there a co-payment on this policy?**
No co-payment applies for entry ages up to 60 years. A 20% co-payment applies for policies where the insured's age at entry is above 60 years.

**Q9. How is the Critical Illness Benefit paid?**
It is a lump sum benefit (50% of Base Sum Insured, capped at ₹10,00,000), paid once, on first diagnosis of a listed illness, subject to a 30-day survival period.

**Q10. Are pre-existing conditions ever covered?**
Yes, after 36 continuous months of coverage under this policy (or a ported predecessor policy) without a break, pre-existing conditions declared at inception become covered.

**Q11. What is the waiting period for accidents?**
None. Accidental injuries are covered from Day 1 of the policy, without any waiting period.

**Q12. Can I increase my Sum Insured at renewal?**
Yes, subject to underwriting approval and possibly a fresh waiting period applicable only to the increased portion of the Sum Insured.

**Q13. What is Hospital Cash Benefit?**
A fixed daily cash payout (₹1,500/day) for each day of hospitalization beyond the first day, up to 15 days per year, payable regardless of actual hospital expenses.

**Q14. Will my premium increase every year?**
Premiums are revised based on age-band changes and any Company-wide repricing approved by IRDAI; individual claims history alone does not increase premium, but the No Claim Bonus and Cumulative Bonus may affect Sum Insured and hence premium calculations at renewal.

**Q15. What documents are needed to add a rider later?**
A written request/endorsement application along with the additional premium; some riders may require fresh health declarations.

**Q16. Is there an age limit for renewal?**
No. This policy offers lifelong renewability, subject to timely premium payment and disclosure requirements.

**Q17. What is the Free Look Period?**
A 15-day window from receipt of the policy document to review the terms and cancel if unsatisfactory, with proportionate deductions as applicable.

**Q18. Does the policy cover day care procedures like cataract surgery?**
Yes, day care procedures not requiring 24-hour hospitalization due to technological advancement are covered under Section 6.3.

**Q19. What happens to my Cumulative Bonus if I make a claim?**
The Cumulative Bonus reduces by 10% for the following year for each year in which a claim is made, but the base Sum Insured is never reduced below the originally opted amount.

**Q20. Whom do I contact for a claim-related grievance?**
You may contact the Company's Grievance Redressal Officer via the channels listed in Section 15 (Customer Support), or escalate to the Insurance Ombudsman if unresolved within 15 days.

**Q21. Can I cover my spouse under the same policy?**
No, this is a single-life individual policy. Each family member requires a separate individual policy, or the Family Health Suraksha floater plan may be considered.

**Q22. Is COVID-19 or similar pandemic illness covered?**
Yes, hospitalization due to any illness, including notified pandemic illnesses, is covered as per standard policy terms, subject to applicable waiting periods and exclusions.

| **Migration** | The right of a policyholder to switch between different products of the same insurer while retaining accrued continuity benefits. |
