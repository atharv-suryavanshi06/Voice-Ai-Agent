# 3. About the Policy

**VitalCare Family Health Shield (VC/FHS/2026)** is an indemnity-based health insurance plan designed to cover hospitalisation expenses, day-care procedures, and related medical costs for the Insured Person(s), on an individual or family floater basis.

The plan is built around the philosophy that a health insurance policy should be simple to understand, wide in coverage, and fast at the time of claim. Key design principles include:

- Wide network of empanelled hospitals offering cashless treatment across India.
- No sub-limit on room rent under the Gold and Platinum variants.
- Long-term policy options (1, 2, and 3 years) with corresponding premium discounts.
- Restoration of Sum Insured in case it is exhausted during the policy year.
- Cumulative No Claim Bonus that rewards claim-free years.

This Policy Document, the Policy Schedule, the Customer Information Sheet, the Proposal Form, and any endorsements together form the complete contract between the Policyholder and VitalCare Health Insurance Pvt. Ltd.

# 8. Benefits

### 8.1 In-Patient Hospitalisation Cover
Covers room rent, nursing charges, ICU charges, doctor's fees, surgeon's fees, anaesthesia, blood, oxygen, operation theatre charges, medicines, and diagnostic tests for hospitalisation exceeding 24 hours, up to the Sum Insured (plus any accrued Restoration or No Claim Bonus).

### 8.2 Pre-Hospitalisation & Post-Hospitalisation Cover
- **Pre-Hospitalisation:** Medical expenses incurred up to 60 days prior to admission, related to the same illness/injury.
- **Post-Hospitalisation:** Medical expenses incurred up to 180 days after discharge, related to the same illness/injury.

### 8.3 Day Care Procedures
Covers medical/surgical procedures that, due to technological advancement, do not require 24-hour hospitalisation (e.g., cataract surgery, dialysis, chemotherapy, lithotripsy), from a list of 550+ procedures specified in the Policy Schedule.

### 8.4 Restoration Benefit
If the Sum Insured (including accrued No Claim Bonus) is exhausted during the Policy Year due to one or more claims, the Sum Insured is automatically restored by 100%, up to unlimited times in a Policy Year, for use towards subsequent unrelated or related illnesses/injuries of any Insured Person under the floater, subject to policy terms.

### 8.5 No Claim Bonus (Cumulative Bonus)
For every claim-free year, the Sum Insured increases by 10% (Silver/Gold) up to a maximum of 100% of the base Sum Insured, without any increase in premium. In the event of a claim, the Cumulative Bonus reduces by 10% at the next renewal, but the base Sum Insured always remains protected.

### 8.6 AYUSH Treatment Cover
In-patient treatment taken under Ayurveda, Yoga & Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, and Homeopathy (AYUSH) systems of medicine, at a registered AYUSH hospital, is covered up to the full Sum Insured.

### 8.7 Health Check-up Benefit
A free annual health check-up is provided for each Insured Person, irrespective of claims made in the previous year, at network diagnostic centres, up to limits specified in the Policy Schedule.

### 8.8 Ambulance Cover
Road ambulance expenses are covered up to ₹3,000 per hospitalisation. Air Ambulance cover (up to ₹5,00,000 per policy year) is available under the Platinum variant.

### 8.9 Optional Covers / Add-ons

| Add-on | Description |
|---|---|
| Personal Accident Cover | Additional lump sum benefit on accidental death/disability of the Insured Person |
| OPD Care Add-on | Reimbursement of outpatient consultation, pharmacy, and diagnostic expenses up to a specified annual limit |
| Critical Illness Booster | Additional lump sum on diagnosis of a listed critical illness, over and above the base Sum Insured |
| Maternity & New-born Cover | Covers maternity expenses and new-born baby cover after a specified waiting period, as an optional add-on |

# 11. Claim Process

### 11.1 Cashless Claim Process (Network Hospital)

1. Identify a VitalCare network hospital (list available on the website/app/helpline).
2. Produce the VitalCare Health Card and photo ID at the hospital's insurance desk.
3. Hospital submits the Pre-Authorisation Request Form to VitalCare's Claims Team.
4. VitalCare reviews and communicates the pre-authorisation decision, typically within 1 hour of receipt of complete information, in line with IRDAI cashless claim turnaround guidelines.
5. Upon discharge, the hospital submits the final bill for cashless settlement directly with VitalCare, subject to policy terms.

### 11.2 Reimbursement Claim Process (Non-Network Hospital)

1. Intimate VitalCare within 24 hours of emergency admission (or prior to admission for planned hospitalisation).
2. Pay hospital bills directly and collect all original bills, reports, and discharge summary.
3. Submit the duly filled Claim Form along with all original documents within 15 days of discharge.
4. VitalCare's Claims Team reviews the documents and may seek clarification or additional documents, if required.
5. On approval, the claim amount is reimbursed directly to the Policyholder's registered bank account.

### 11.3 Claim Settlement Timelines
- Cashless pre-authorisation decision: within 1 hour of receipt of complete information.
- Final cashless bill settlement after discharge: within 3 hours of receipt of complete discharge documents, wherever feasible.
- Reimbursement claims: settled within 15 days of receipt of all required documents, as per applicable regulatory timelines.

# 13. Premium Payment Rules

### 13.1 Grace Period
A Grace Period of **30 days** is allowed for renewal of the Policy after the expiry date, during which continuity benefits (waiting period credit, cumulative bonus, moratorium period) are preserved, though coverage remains suspended until premium is paid and the Policy is renewed.

### 13.2 Renewal
The Policy is renewable for life, subject to timely payment of premium, and VitalCare shall not deny renewal on the sole ground of age or claims made in previous years, except on grounds of established fraud or moral hazard.

### 13.3 Free Look Period
The Policyholder may review this Policy within **30 days** of receipt of the Policy Document, and may cancel it if not agreeable to the terms, provided no claim has been made, subject to deduction of proportionate risk premium for the period on cover and any expenses incurred by the Company (e.g., medical check-up costs).

### 13.4 Cancellation & Refund
- The Policyholder may cancel the Policy at any time by providing written notice, subject to a refund of premium for the unexpired policy period, on a short-period scale, provided no claim has been made during the policy year.
- VitalCare may cancel the Policy on grounds of established fraud or non-disclosure, by giving 15 days' written notice, subject to applicable law.

### 13.5 Portability
Policyholders may port this Policy to another insurer, or migrate to another VitalCare product, at the time of renewal, subject to IRDAI portability guidelines. Continuity benefits (waiting periods served, moratorium period) shall be carried forward to the extent of the Sum Insured under the previous policy.

# 15. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1. What is VitalCare Family Health Shield?**
It is an indemnity-based health insurance plan that covers hospitalisation, day-care, and related medical expenses for individuals or the entire family under a single floater Sum Insured.

**Q2. What does "Family Floater" mean?**
Under a floater policy, a single Sum Insured is shared across all Insured Members of the family, and can be utilised by any one or more members in a policy year.

**Q3. Is there a cap on room rent?**
Under the Gold and Platinum variants, there is no capping on room rent. Under the Silver variant, room rent is capped at a Single Private A/C Room.

**Q4. What is the Restoration Benefit?**
If the Sum Insured is exhausted during the policy year, it is automatically restored by 100%, allowing continued coverage for further claims in the same year.

**Q5. How is the No Claim Bonus calculated?**
For every claim-free year, the Sum Insured increases by 10%, up to a maximum of 100% of the base Sum Insured, at no additional premium.

**Q6. What is the waiting period for pre-existing diseases?**
36 months of continuous coverage from the date of first inception of the policy with VitalCare, as per applicable IRDAI norms.

**Q7. Are day-care procedures covered?**
Yes, over 550 day-care procedures that do not require 24-hour hospitalisation are covered under this Policy.

**Q8. Is AYUSH treatment covered?**
Yes, in-patient AYUSH treatment at a registered AYUSH hospital is covered up to the Sum Insured.

**Q9. How do I avail cashless treatment?**
Visit any VitalCare network hospital, present your Health Card and ID, and the hospital will coordinate pre-authorisation directly with VitalCare.

**Q10. What is the turnaround time for cashless pre-authorisation?**
As per IRDAI guidelines, cashless pre-authorisation decisions are typically communicated within 1 hour of receipt of complete information from the hospital.

**Q11. Can I get treatment at a non-network hospital?**
Yes, you may opt for reimbursement claims at non-network hospitals by paying upfront and submitting documents for reimbursement.

**Q12. What is the moratorium period?**
After 5 years of continuous coverage, VitalCare cannot reject a claim on grounds of non-disclosure or misrepresentation, except in cases of proven fraud, as per IRDAI regulations.

**Q13. Can senior citizens buy this policy?**
Yes, there is no maximum entry age under this Policy, in line with IRDAI's lifetime renewability and age-inclusivity guidelines. A co-payment may apply for entry age 61 and above under the Silver variant.

**Q14. What happens if I don't renew on time?**
A Grace Period of 30 days is provided for renewal. Coverage remains suspended during this period, and continuity benefits are protected only if renewed within the Grace Period.

**Q15. Can I port my existing health policy to VitalCare?**
Yes, subject to IRDAI portability guidelines, you may port an existing health policy to VitalCare at the time of renewal, carrying forward applicable continuity benefits.

**Q16. What is covered under Pre and Post Hospitalisation?**
Medical expenses incurred 60 days before admission and 180 days after discharge, related to the same illness/injury, are covered.

**Q17. Is maternity covered under the base plan?**
No, maternity is available only as an optional add-on, subject to a 36-month waiting period and applicable sub-limits.

**Q18. How do I add a new-born baby to my policy?**
The new-born can be added at the next renewal, subject to the Maternity & New-born Cover add-on being opted for and the applicable waiting period being met.

**Q19. What is not covered under this Policy?**
Cosmetic treatments, dental treatment (unless due to accident), self-inflicted injuries, and treatment outside India (unless specifically covered) are among the key exclusions. Refer to Section 10 for the complete list.

**Q20. How can I check my claim status?**
Claim status can be tracked through the VitalCare mobile app, customer portal, or by contacting the toll-free helpline with your claim reference number.

**Q21. Is a free health check-up available?**
Yes, an annual health check-up is provided to each Insured Person, irrespective of whether a claim was made in the previous year.

**Q22. What tax benefit can I claim on my premium?**
Premiums qualify for deduction under Section 80D of the Income Tax Act, 1961, subject to prescribed limits based on the age of the insured members.

**Q23. Can I increase my Sum Insured at renewal?**
Yes, Sum Insured enhancement can be requested at renewal, subject to underwriting; a fresh waiting period shall apply only to the incremental Sum Insured.

**Q24. What happens to my Cumulative Bonus if I make a claim?**
The Cumulative Bonus reduces by 10% at the next renewal for each policy year in which a claim is made, but the base Sum Insured is never reduced below the originally opted amount.
