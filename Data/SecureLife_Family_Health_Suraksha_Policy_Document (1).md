## 1. About the Policy

SecureLife Family Health Suraksha is a family floater, indemnity-based health insurance plan underwritten by SecureLife Insurance Pvt. Ltd., designed to cover an entire family — the Proposer, spouse, dependent children, and optionally dependent parents — under a single, shared Sum Insured.

Unlike an individual plan where each person has a separate Sum Insured, a floater plan allows any one or more insured family members to draw upon the same pool of Sum Insured in a policy year, up to the total limit. This plan places particular emphasis on maternity, newborn baby cover, and family preventive wellness, making it suitable for young families planning to have or already raising children.

This document forms part of the policy contract and should be read together with the Policy Schedule, Proposal Form, and any endorsements issued by the Company.

## 6. Benefits

### 6.1 Hospitalization Benefit (Base Cover)
Covers Room Rent, Nursing Charges, ICU Charges, Doctor's Fees, Surgeon's Fees, Anaesthesia, Blood, Oxygen, OT Charges, Medicines, and Investigations for In-patient treatment exceeding 24 hours, for any insured family member, drawn from the shared floater Sum Insured.

### 6.2 Pre and Post-Hospitalization Benefit
- Pre-hospitalization medical expenses covered for 30 days immediately before admission.
- Post-hospitalization medical expenses covered for 60 days immediately after discharge.

### 6.3 Day Care Treatment
All medical/surgical procedures requiring less than 24 hours of hospitalization due to technological advancement are covered for all insured members, as per the Company's list of 150+ Day Care Procedures.

### 6.4 Maternity Benefit
Covers hospitalization expenses for delivery (normal or caesarean) of the insured woman (Proposer or spouse), subject to:
- A waiting period of 36 continuous months from the date of first inception of this policy (or a predecessor floater policy with the Company, without a break).
- Sub-limits as specified in Section 5 (₹75,000 for normal delivery; ₹1,00,000 for caesarean).
- Limited to two live births/deliveries during the lifetime of the policy, per insured woman.
- Complications of pregnancy requiring hospitalization are covered as part of the maternity sub-limit.

### 6.5 New Born Baby Cover
A newly born child is automatically covered from Day 1 of birth (without a separate premium) until the next policy renewal, for medical expenses relating to the newborn's illness or congenital condition requiring hospitalization, up to ₹1,00,000 within the overall floater Sum Insured. The child must be formally added to the policy at the next renewal (with applicable additional premium) to continue coverage thereafter.

### 6.6 Vaccination Cover
Reimbursement of routine vaccination expenses for a covered newborn/infant up to the age of 1 year, as per the standard immunization schedule, subject to a sub-limit of ₹15,000.

### 6.7 Multiplier Benefit (Floater Enhancement)
If the family's cumulative claims in a policy year are less than 25% of the Family Floater Sum Insured, the Sum Insured increases by 10% at the next renewal, up to a maximum of 50% additional cover, at no extra premium — benefiting the entire family pool rather than any single member.

### 6.8 Annual Family Health Check-up
One preventive health check-up per policy year for up to 2 adult members of the family (Proposer and spouse), covering routine blood work, ECG, and physician consultation, at Network Diagnostic Centres, irrespective of whether a claim has been made.

### 6.9 Automatic Family Inclusion
- A newly married spouse can be added mid-term within 90 days of marriage, subject to a pro-rata premium and applicable waiting periods commencing afresh for the new member.
- A newborn child is automatically covered as per Section 6.5 and must be formally endorsed at renewal.

### 6.10 Optional Add-on Modules (Available on Payment of Additional Premium)
| Add-on Name | Description |
|---|---|
| Parental Cover Module | Extends floater-style cover to dependent parents (up to age 75) under a separately calculated sub-limit within the same policy |

| Infertility Treatment Rider | Covers IVF and related fertility treatments up to ₹2,00,000, after a 4-year waiting period |

## 7. Exclusions

The following are **not covered** under this policy, unless specifically stated otherwise:

- Pre-existing Diseases, until 36 months of continuous coverage have elapsed (Waiting Period)
- Specific illnesses (e.g., cataract, hernia, hysterectomy, joint replacement, varicose veins) for the first 24 months of the policy (Specific Waiting Period)
- Maternity expenses within the first 36 months of the policy (see Section 6.4)
- Any claim within the first 30 days of policy inception, except for Accidental Hospitalization
- **Critical Illness diagnoses of any kind (including but not limited to Cancer, Heart Attack, Kidney Failure, Stroke) are not covered as a standalone lump-sum benefit under this plan; only the underlying hospitalization expense, if otherwise admissible, may be reimbursed under the base hospitalization benefit, subject to policy terms.**
- Cosmetic or plastic surgery, unless necessitated by an Accident
- Dental treatment or surgery, unless requiring hospitalization due to an Accident
- Infertility treatment, artificial insemination, and related procedures, unless the Infertility Treatment Rider has been opted for
- Voluntary termination of pregnancy within the first 12 weeks, except where medically necessitated
- Self-inflicted injury, attempted suicide, or intentional self-harm
- Injury or illness arising from use of alcohol, drugs, or intoxicating substances
- Participation in adventure sports, professional sports, or hazardous activities
- War, invasion, act of foreign enemy, civil war, nuclear, chemical, or biological attack
- Treatment taken outside India
- Experimental, unproven, or investigational treatment not recognized by the medical council
- Vitamins, tonics, and general debility treatment not related to a diagnosed illness
- Expenses on spectacles, contact lenses, hearing aids, and durable medical equipment unless specified in a rider

## 8. Claim Process

### 8.1 Cashless Claim Process
1. Identify a Network Hospital for planned or emergency treatment of any covered family member.
2. Present the Family Health Card and photo ID of the insured member at the hospital's insurance desk.
3. Hospital submits a pre-authorization request to the Company/TPA, specifying which insured member is being treated.
4. Company/TPA reviews and issues approval (or query), typically within 4 hours for emergency cases and 48 hours before admission for planned/maternity cases.
5. On discharge, the hospital settles the bill directly with the Company/TPA, subject to policy terms, sub-limits, and any non-payable items. The claim amount is deducted from the shared family floater Sum Insured.

### 8.2 Reimbursement Claim Process
1. Intimate the claim to the Company within 48 hours of emergency admission, or 48 hours prior for planned admission/maternity.
2. Undergo treatment and pay the hospital bills directly.
3. Submit the duly filled claim form (specifying the insured member treated) along with original bills, reports, and discharge summary within 30 days of discharge.
4. Company/TPA reviews documents; may request additional information within 7 working days of receipt.
5. Claim is settled within 15 days of receipt of last necessary document, as per applicable regulatory timelines.

### 8.3 Maternity / New Born Claim Process
1. Intimate the expected delivery date at least 15 days in advance for planned deliveries, where possible.
2. On admission, follow the cashless or reimbursement process as above, specifying "Maternity" as the claim category.
3. For newborn-specific complications, raise the claim under the New Born Baby Cover, referencing the mother's policy details, within 30 days of discharge.
4. Formally endorse the newborn onto the policy at the next renewal to continue coverage beyond the current policy year.

## 13. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1. How is a family floater policy different from an individual policy?**
In a floater policy, all covered family members share a single Sum Insured, which any member can draw upon (fully or partially) in a given policy year. In an individual policy, each person has an independent, separate Sum Insured.

**Q2. Does this plan cover Critical Illnesses like Cancer?**
No. This plan does not include a Critical Illness Benefit or Cancer-specific lump sum payout. Only the hospitalization expense arising from any covered illness, if otherwise admissible under the base hospitalization benefit, may be reimbursed. Customers seeking dedicated Critical Illness/Cancer cover should consider SecureLife Individual Health Shield (SIHS-2026).

**Q3. When does Maternity cover start?**
Maternity Benefit becomes payable only after 36 continuous months of coverage under this policy (or a ported/migrated predecessor policy without a break).

**Q4. Is a newborn automatically covered?**
Yes, from Day 1 of birth until the next renewal, up to ₹1,00,000 for illness/congenital conditions requiring hospitalization. The child must be formally added at the next renewal, with additional premium, to continue cover thereafter.

**Q5. Can I add my parents to this floater policy?**
Yes, via the optional Parental Cover Module, subject to a maximum entry age of 75 years and a separately calculated sub-limit within the same policy.

**Q6. What is the Multiplier Benefit?**
If the family's total claims in a year are less than 25% of the Sum Insured, the floater Sum Insured increases by 10% at renewal (up to 50% cumulative), benefiting the whole family, not just one member.

**Q7. Can I choose any hospital for cashless treatment?**
Cashless facility is available only at hospitals within the Company's Network Hospital list. Treatment at a non-network hospital can be claimed on a reimbursement basis.

**Q8. Is co-payment applicable on this policy?**
No co-payment applies for members up to age 60. A 20% co-payment applies for any covered dependent parent aged 61 years or above.

**Q9. What happens if the floater Sum Insured is exhausted by one family member's claim?**
The remaining balance (if any) is available to other family members for the rest of the policy year; if fully exhausted, no further claims can be made until the Multiplier Benefit or renewal replenishes the Sum Insured.

**Q10. Does this plan cover normal delivery and C-section equally?**
Both are covered, subject to different sub-limits: ₹75,000 for normal delivery and ₹1,00,000 for caesarean section, within the overall floater Sum Insured, after the waiting period.

**Q11. Is infertility treatment covered?**
Not under the base plan. It is available only if the optional Infertility Treatment Rider is purchased, subject to a 4-year waiting period and a ₹2,00,000 sub-limit.

**Q12. Can I port my existing family floater policy from another insurer to this plan?**
Yes, subject to IRDAI Portability Guidelines. Credit for waiting periods already served (including maternity waiting period, if applicable and disclosed) is considered, provided the port request is made 45–60 days before the previous policy's renewal date.

**Q13. What is the Annual Family Health Check-up benefit?**
One preventive health check-up per policy year for up to 2 adult members, covering routine blood work, ECG, and physician consultation, regardless of whether a claim has been made during the year.

**Q14. What if my child turns 25 during the policy period?**
The child ceases to be eligible as a dependent from the following renewal and must be moved to an independent individual policy.

**Q15. How many deliveries are covered under the Maternity Benefit?**
Up to two live births/deliveries per insured woman, during the lifetime of the policy, subject to policy terms.

**Q16. What is the waiting period for accidents?**
None. Accidental injuries are covered from Day 1 of the policy for all insured members, without any waiting period.

**Q17. Does the Long-Term Discount apply to all premium modes?**
The 10% (2-year) / 15% (3-year) Long-Term Discount applies only where the full multi-year premium is paid upfront in a single payment at inception.

**Q18. Can I remove a family member mid-term?**
A family member can be removed from the floater only in specific circumstances (e.g., independent policy taken elsewhere, marriage of a dependent), through a written endorsement request; premium is adjusted on a short-period basis.

**Q19. What is the Free Look Period?**
A 15-day window from receipt of the policy document to review the terms and cancel if unsatisfactory, with proportionate deductions as applicable.

**Q20. Whom do I contact for a claim-related grievance?**
You may contact the Company's Grievance Redressal Officer via the channels listed in Section 15 (Customer Support), or escalate to the Insurance Ombudsman if unresolved within 15 days.

**Q21. Is vaccination cover available for older children too?**
No, the Vaccination Cover sub-limit under Section 6.6 applies only to a covered newborn/infant up to age 1, as part of the New Born Baby Cover.

**Q22. If I want both maternity and critical illness cover, what should I do?**
You would need to hold this Family Health Suraksha policy (for maternity/family cover) alongside a separate SecureLife Individual Health Shield (SIHS-2026) policy for the relevant individual, since the two products are not combined in a single policy.
