# 3. About the Policy

**WellNest Total HealthCare (WN/THC/2026)** is a wellness-linked, indemnity-based health insurance plan that combines comprehensive hospitalisation cover with everyday health benefits such as outpatient (OPD) consultations, teleconsultations, and a reward-linked wellness programme, on an individual or family floater basis.

The plan reflects a preventive-care philosophy, encouraging policyholders to actively manage their health year-round, not only at the time of hospitalisation. Key design elements include:

- No room rent capping across all variants — policyholders may choose any room category.
- A built-in Wellness Programme that rewards healthy habits (steps tracked, health assessments completed) with premium discounts at renewal.
- Unlimited teleconsultations with network doctors, included in the base plan.
- Flexible OPD cover as an in-built or optional benefit, depending on variant.

This Policy Document, the Policy Schedule, the Customer Information Sheet, the Proposal Form, and any endorsements together form the complete contract between the Policyholder and WellNest Health Insurance Ltd.

# 8. Benefits

### 8.1 In-Patient Hospitalisation Cover
Covers room rent (no capping), nursing charges, ICU charges, surgeon's and doctor's fees, anaesthesia, operation theatre charges, medicines, and diagnostic tests for hospitalisation exceeding 24 hours.

### 8.2 Pre-Hospitalisation & Post-Hospitalisation Cover
- **Essential Variant:** 30 days pre-hospitalisation and 60 days post-hospitalisation.
- **Premier Variant:** 60 days pre-hospitalisation and 180 days post-hospitalisation.

### 8.3 Day Care Procedures
Covers medical/surgical procedures not requiring 24-hour hospitalisation due to technological advancement, from a specified list included in the Policy Schedule.

### 8.4 Restoration Benefit
- **Essential Variant:** Sum Insured restored 100% once per Policy Year, on exhaustion due to a claim, for subsequent unrelated illness/injury.
- **Premier Variant:** Sum Insured restored 100%, unlimited number of times per Policy Year, for any Insured Person under the floater.

### 8.5 OPD & Teleconsultation Cover
- **Premier Variant:** In-built OPD cover up to ₹15,000 per Policy Year for doctor consultations, diagnostic tests, and pharmacy expenses, subject to sub-limits specified in the Policy Schedule.
- **Essential Variant:** OPD cover available only as an optional add-on.
- **All Variants:** Unlimited teleconsultations with WellNest's network of empanelled doctors are included at no extra cost.

### 8.6 Wellness Programme & Renewal Discount
Insured Persons can earn Wellness Points by completing annual health risk assessments, maintaining tracked physical activity levels (via the WellNest App), and undergoing preventive screenings. Accumulated Wellness Points are converted into a renewal premium discount of up to 20%, subject to the terms of the Wellness Programme published on the WellNest website.

### 8.7 AYUSH Treatment Cover
In-patient treatment under Ayurveda, Yoga & Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, and Homeopathy (AYUSH) systems, taken at a registered AYUSH hospital, is covered up to the Sum Insured.

### 8.8 Annual Health Check-up
A complimentary annual health check-up is available for all Insured Persons under the Premier variant, and for Insured Persons in the Essential variant after one claim-free year.

### 8.9 Ambulance Cover
Road ambulance expenses are covered up to ₹2,500 per hospitalisation event under the Essential variant, and up to ₹5,000 under the Premier variant.

### 8.10 Optional Covers / Add-ons

| Add-on | Description |
|---|---|
| OPD Cover (Essential variant only) | Reimbursement of outpatient consultation, diagnostic, and pharmacy expenses up to a specified annual limit |
| Personal Accident Cover | Additional lump sum benefit on accidental death/disability of the Insured Person |

# 11. Claim Process

### 11.1 Cashless Claim Process (Network Hospital)

1. Locate a WellNest network hospital via the app, website, or helpline.
2. Present the WellNest Health Card and a valid photo ID at the hospital's insurance desk.
3. The hospital submits a Pre-Authorisation Request to WellNest's Claims Team.
4. WellNest reviews and communicates its decision, typically within 1 hour of receipt of complete information, in line with IRDAI cashless claim turnaround guidelines.
5. On discharge, the hospital submits the final bill directly to WellNest for settlement, subject to policy terms.

### 11.2 Reimbursement Claim Process (Non-Network Hospital)

1. Intimate WellNest within 24 hours of emergency admission (or prior to admission for planned hospitalisation).
2. Settle hospital bills directly and retain all original bills, reports, and the discharge summary.
3. Submit the completed Claim Form along with all original supporting documents within 15 days of discharge.
4. WellNest's Claims Team reviews the submission and may request additional clarification or documents, if required.
5. On approval, the eligible claim amount is credited directly to the Policyholder's registered bank account.

### 11.3 OPD / Teleconsultation Claim Process
- Teleconsultations booked through the WellNest App are settled directly with the network doctor at no cost to the Insured Person.
- OPD reimbursement claims (where applicable) must be submitted through the WellNest App along with original bills and prescriptions within 30 days of the consultation/purchase.

### 11.4 Claim Settlement Timelines
- Cashless pre-authorisation decision: within 1 hour of receipt of complete information.
- Final cashless bill settlement: within 3 hours of receipt of complete discharge documents, wherever feasible.
- Reimbursement claims (hospitalisation and OPD): settled within 15 days of receipt of all required documents.

# 13. Premium Payment Rules

### 13.1 Grace Period
A Grace Period of **30 days** is allowed for renewal after the Policy expiry date, during which continuity benefits (waiting period credit, moratorium period, accrued Wellness Points) are preserved, though coverage remains suspended until renewal premium is paid.

### 13.2 Renewal
The Policy is renewable for life, subject to timely payment of premium. WellNest shall not deny renewal on the sole ground of age or past claims, except on grounds of established fraud or moral hazard, in accordance with IRDAI guidelines.

### 13.3 Free Look Period
The Policyholder may review this Policy within **30 days** of receipt of the Policy Document and may cancel it if not agreeable to the terms, provided no claim has been made, subject to deduction of proportionate risk premium for the period on cover and any expenses already incurred by the Company.

### 13.4 Cancellation & Refund
- The Policyholder may cancel the Policy at any time with written notice, and shall be entitled to a refund of premium for the unexpired period on a short-period scale, provided no claim has been made during the Policy Year.
- WellNest may cancel the Policy on grounds of established fraud or non-disclosure, subject to 15 days' prior written notice and applicable law.

### 13.5 Portability
Policyholders may port this Policy to another insurer, or migrate to another WellNest product, at renewal, subject to IRDAI portability guidelines, carrying forward continuity benefits up to the Sum Insured of the previous policy.

# 15. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1. What is WellNest Total HealthCare?**
It is a wellness-linked health insurance plan combining comprehensive hospitalisation cover with everyday benefits like OPD consultations, teleconsultation, and a rewards-based Wellness Programme.

**Q2. What is the Wellness Programme?**
It is a rewards system under which Insured Persons earn Wellness Points for completing health assessments and maintaining tracked activity levels, redeemable for up to a 20% discount on renewal premium.

**Q3. Is OPD cover included in the base plan?**
Under the Premier variant, OPD cover up to ₹15,000 per Policy Year is in-built. Under the Essential variant, OPD cover is available only as an optional add-on.

**Q4. Are teleconsultations free?**
Yes, unlimited teleconsultations with WellNest's network doctors are included in the base plan across all variants, at no extra cost.

**Q5. Is there a cap on room rent?**
No, there is no room rent capping under either the Essential or Premier variant.

**Q6. What is the Restoration Benefit under this plan?**
Under the Premier variant, the Sum Insured is restored 100%, unlimited times per Policy Year. Under the Essential variant, restoration is available once per Policy Year.

**Q7. What is the waiting period for pre-existing diseases?**
36 months of continuous coverage from the date of first inception of the policy with WellNest, as per applicable IRDAI norms.

**Q8. How do I earn Wellness Points?**
By completing the annual health risk assessment, syncing activity data via the WellNest App, and undergoing recommended preventive screenings.

**Q9. Can Wellness Points be redeemed for cash?**
No, Wellness Points can only be redeemed as a discount on the renewal premium and have no cash value.

**Q10. How do I avail cashless treatment?**
Visit any WellNest network hospital, present your Health Card and photo ID, and the hospital will coordinate pre-authorisation directly with WellNest.

**Q11. What is the turnaround time for cashless approval?**
As per IRDAI guidelines, cashless pre-authorisation decisions are typically communicated within 1 hour of receipt of complete information from the hospital.

**Q12. Can I claim for treatment at a non-network hospital?**
Yes, through the reimbursement process; pay the hospital directly and submit required documents within 15 days of discharge.

**Q13. What is the moratorium period?**
After 5 years of continuous coverage, WellNest cannot reject a claim on grounds of non-disclosure or misrepresentation, except where fraud is established.

**Q14. Is there a maximum entry age for this policy?**
No, there is no maximum entry age, in line with IRDAI's lifetime renewability guidelines.

**Q15. What happens if I don't renew on time?**
A Grace Period of 30 days is provided. Coverage remains suspended during this period; continuity benefits, including accrued Wellness Points, are preserved only if renewed within the Grace Period.

**Q16. Can I port my existing health policy to WellNest?**
Yes, subject to IRDAI portability guidelines, at the time of renewal of your existing policy.

**Q17. What is covered under Pre and Post Hospitalisation?**
Under the Premier variant, 60 days pre-hospitalisation and 180 days post-hospitalisation are covered; under the Essential variant, the periods are 30 and 60 days respectively.

**Q18. Is maternity covered under the base plan?**
No, maternity is available only as an optional add-on, subject to a 36-month waiting period and applicable sub-limits.

**Q19. What is not covered under this Policy?**
Cosmetic treatments, dental treatment (unless due to accident), self-inflicted injuries, and OPD expenses beyond applicable sub-limits are among the key exclusions. Refer to Section 10 for the full list.

**Q20. How can I track my claim status?**
Through the WellNest mobile app, customer portal, or by contacting the toll-free helpline with your claim reference number.

**Q21. Is a free health check-up available?**
Yes, under the Premier variant it is available every year; under the Essential variant it is available after one claim-free year.

**Q22. What tax benefit can I claim on my premium?**
Premiums qualify for deduction under Section 80D of the Income Tax Act, 1961, subject to prescribed limits based on the age of the insured members.

**Q23. Can I switch from the Essential to the Premier variant?**
Yes, variant upgrades can be requested at renewal, subject to underwriting; a fresh waiting period applies only to the incremental Sum Insured or newly added benefits.

**Q24. Do Wellness Points expire?**
Yes, Wellness Points accumulated in a Policy Year must be redeemed at the immediately following renewal and do not carry forward beyond that renewal cycle.
