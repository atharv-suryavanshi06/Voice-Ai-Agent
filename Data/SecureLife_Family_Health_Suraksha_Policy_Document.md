## 3. About the Policy

SecureLife Family Health Suraksha is a **family floater, indemnity-based health insurance plan** that provides comprehensive hospitalisation cover to the entire family under a single Sum Insured, which can be utilised by any one or more family members during the Policy Year. The plan is designed for families who want holistic protection against rising healthcare costs — covering everything from planned surgeries and day care procedures to serious illnesses such as **cancer**, requiring extended chemotherapy or radiotherapy, as well as maternity and newborn baby cover.

Unlike a fixed-benefit plan, this policy works on a **reimbursement/cashless indemnity basis**, meaning the Company pays actual, reasonable, and customary medical expenses incurred, up to the Sum Insured, subject to policy terms, sub-limits, and applicable waiting periods. The plan includes a **Restoration Benefit** and a **No Claim Bonus** to ensure the family's protection scales with their needs over time.

This is a **health indemnity offering** of SecureLife Insurance Pvt. Ltd.'s Health Division, distinct from the Company's life insurance and fixed-benefit critical illness products.

## 8. Benefits

### 8.1 In-Patient Hospitalisation Cover

Covers room rent, ICU charges, doctor's and surgeon's fees, anaesthesia, blood, oxygen, operation theatre charges, medicines, and diagnostic tests for hospitalisation exceeding 24 continuous hours, up to the Sum Insured.

### 8.2 Cancer Treatment Cover

This plan **covers hospitalisation and treatment expenses arising from cancer of any organ or system**, including but not limited to:

- Surgical oncology procedures (tumour removal, mastectomy, etc.).
- Chemotherapy, including day care chemotherapy sessions not requiring overnight admission.
- Radiotherapy, including external beam radiotherapy and brachytherapy.
- Targeted therapy and immunotherapy administered under medical supervision.
- Associated hospitalisation for complications arising from cancer treatment.

Cancer treatment is covered as part of the overall Sum Insured, subject to the Waiting Period specified in Section 8.9 and the exclusions specified in Section 9.

### 8.3 Day Care Treatment

Covers 180+ listed Day Care Procedures (e.g., cataract surgery, dialysis, chemotherapy, tonsillectomy) that do not require 24-hour hospitalisation due to technological advancement, subject to the full Sum Insured.

### 8.4 Pre- and Post-Hospitalisation Expenses

- **Pre-hospitalisation:** Medical expenses incurred up to 60 days before admission, related to the same illness/injury.
- **Post-hospitalisation:** Medical expenses incurred up to 180 days after discharge, related to the same illness/injury.

### 8.5 Maternity Benefit and New Born Baby Cover

- Covers normal delivery and caesarean section expenses, subject to a 36-month waiting period from the first policy inception and a sub-limit of ₹75,000 for normal delivery and ₹1,00,000 for caesarean delivery, per delivery, limited to two deliveries during the lifetime of the policy.
- New Born Baby Cover is available from Day 1 of birth up to the end of the Policy Year, including cover for congenital conditions, subject to sub-limits specified in the Policy Schedule.

### 8.6 Ambulance Cover

Reimburses road ambulance expenses up to ₹3,000 per hospitalisation event for transport to the nearest hospital with adequate facilities.

### 8.7 AYUSH Treatment Cover

Covers in-patient treatment taken under Ayurveda, Yoga & Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, and Homeopathy systems of medicine, at a recognised AYUSH hospital, up to the Sum Insured.

### 8.8 Annual Health Check-up

A free annual health check-up is available for all insured adult members, regardless of claim history, at network diagnostic centres, as per the Company's applicable check-up package.

### 8.9 Waiting Periods

| Condition | Waiting Period |
|---|---|
| Initial Waiting Period (all illnesses, except accidents) | 30 days from policy inception |
| Pre-Existing Diseases | 36 months of continuous coverage |
| Specific Illnesses (e.g., cataract, hernia, joint replacement, kidney stones, ENT disorders) | 24 months of continuous coverage |
| Maternity Benefit | 36 months of continuous coverage |
| Cancer and other Malignant Conditions (if not pre-existing) | 90 days from policy inception |

### 8.10 Restoration Benefit and No Claim Bonus

- **Restoration Benefit:** If the Sum Insured (plus accrued No Claim Bonus) is exhausted during the Policy Year due to a claim, 100% of the Sum Insured is automatically restored, once per Policy Year, for use on an unrelated illness/injury for any insured member.
- **No Claim Bonus:** Increases the Sum Insured by 10% for every claim-free Policy Year, up to a maximum accumulation of 50%, without any increase in premium.

## 9. Exclusions

### 9.1 Permanent Exclusions

- Cosmetic or plastic surgery, unless required due to accidental injury or reconstructive surgery following a mastectomy for cancer.
- Dental treatment or surgery, unless requiring hospitalisation due to accidental injury.
- Treatment for obesity, weight control, or bariatric surgery, unless medically necessitated and pre-authorised for a co-morbid condition.
- Any treatment related to sterility, infertility, assisted reproduction, and sex change.
- Self-inflicted injury, attempted suicide, or intentional self-harm.
- Treatment arising from use of intoxicating substances, alcohol, or drug abuse.
- War, invasion, act of foreign enemy, hostilities, civil war, nuclear, chemical, or biological contamination.
- Expenses related to unproven or experimental treatment not recognised by the medical council.
- Hospitalisation for investigation/diagnostic purposes only, not followed by active treatment.
- Refractive error correction below 7.5 dioptres.

### 9.2 Temporary/Waiting-Period Exclusions

- Pre-existing diseases, until completion of the 36-month waiting period, unless disclosed and specifically covered by endorsement.
- Specified illnesses listed in Section 8.9, until completion of the applicable waiting period.
- Any claim within the first 30 days of policy inception, except for claims arising from accidental injury.

### 9.3 Cancer-Specific Notes

- Cancer treatment is covered as described in Section 8.2; however, claims for cancer diagnosed and treatment commenced within the first 90 days of the policy (where the condition is later established to be pre-existing) shall be assessed under the Pre-Existing Disease clause and may not be payable until the 36-month waiting period is met.
- Purely experimental or unapproved cancer therapies not recognised by the Medical Council of India/National Medical Commission are excluded.

## 10. Claim Process

### 10.1 Cashless Claim Process

1. **Admission:** Insured member is admitted to a network hospital and produces the SecureLife Health Card and photo ID at the hospital's insurance desk.
2. **Pre-Authorisation Request:** The network hospital submits a Pre-Authorisation Form to the Company's Health Claims Team (directly or via the Company's Third-Party Administrator).
3. **Approval:** The Company reviews the request and issues an initial authorisation letter, typically within 2–4 hours for emergency admissions and within 48 hours for planned admissions.
4. **Treatment & Discharge:** Treatment proceeds cashlessly up to the approved limit; on discharge, the hospital submits the final bill for settlement.
5. **Final Settlement:** The Company settles the final bill directly with the hospital, subject to policy terms, sub-limits, and any non-payable items as per the Policy Schedule.

### 10.2 Reimbursement Claim Process

1. **Intimation:** Intimate the claim within 48 hours of emergency admission (or 48 hours prior to a planned admission) via toll-free number, email, or the online portal.
2. **Treatment:** Undergo treatment at any hospital of choice (network or non-network) and settle the bill directly.
3. **Document Submission:** Submit the duly filled claim form along with original bills, reports, and discharge summary within 15 days of discharge.
4. **Assessment:** The Company's claims team reviews the documents against policy terms and, if required, seeks additional information/clarification.
5. **Settlement:** On approval, the claim amount is reimbursed via NEFT/RTGS to the Policyholder's registered bank account, within 15 days of receipt of complete documentation.

### 10.3 Turnaround Time Summary

| Stage | Standard Timeline |
|---|---|
| Cashless Pre-Authorisation (Emergency) | 2–4 hours |
| Cashless Pre-Authorisation (Planned) | 48 hours |
| Reimbursement Claim Intimation | Within 48 hours of admission/discharge |
| Reimbursement Claim Settlement | Within 15 days of complete documentation |

## 13. Premium Payment Rules

### 13.1 Grace Period

- A Grace Period of **30 days** from the policy expiry date is allowed for renewal of the policy.
- No coverage is available for any illness/hospitalisation event occurring during the Grace Period; continuity benefits (such as waiting period credit) are protected only if the policy is renewed within this Grace Period.

### 13.2 Lapse

- If the policy is not renewed within the Grace Period, the policy **lapses**, and all coverage, including accrued No Claim Bonus and waiting period credits, is forfeited.
- A fresh proposal, subject to fresh underwriting and waiting periods, would be required to obtain cover again.

### 13.3 Revival

- This being an annual indemnity health policy, "revival" is effectively achieved through **timely renewal** within the Grace Period; no separate revival mechanism (as applicable to long-term life policies) exists for this product.

### 13.4 Surrender/Cancellation

- The Policyholder may cancel the policy at any time during the Policy Term by providing written notice to the Company.
- On cancellation, provided no claim has been made during the Policy Year, a pro-rata refund of premium for the unexpired policy period shall be made, subject to a minimum retention period of 1 month's premium by the Company.
- No refund is applicable if a claim has been made and paid/admitted during the Policy Year.

### 13.5 Free Look Period

- Available for policies with a term of 1 year or more, issued for the first time (not applicable at renewal).
- The Policyholder may review the policy within **30 days** of receipt and request cancellation if not satisfied.
- On cancellation within the Free Look Period, the premium shall be refunded after deducting proportionate risk premium for the period of cover, medical examination costs (if any), and stamp duty.

## 15. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1. What does "family floater" mean?**
It means a single Sum Insured (here, ₹10,00,000) is shared across all insured family members, and any member can utilise part or all of it in a Policy Year.

**Q2. Is cancer treatment covered under this policy?**
Yes. Surgery, chemotherapy, radiotherapy, targeted therapy, and immunotherapy for cancer are covered as part of the overall Sum Insured, subject to the applicable waiting period and exclusions.

**Q3. What is the waiting period for cancer claims?**
Generally 90 days from policy inception for newly diagnosed cancer; if the cancer is later established as pre-existing, the 36-month Pre-Existing Disease waiting period applies instead.

**Q4. Can I add my parents to this floater policy?**
Parents and parents-in-law can be added as an optional extension, underwritten separately, typically on a separate floater Sum Insured given the higher age-related risk.

**Q5. What is the Restoration Benefit?**
If your family's Sum Insured is exhausted during the Policy Year due to one claim, 100% of the Sum Insured is automatically restored once, for use towards an unrelated illness/injury.

**Q6. Does the policy cover normal and caesarean delivery?**
Yes, after a 36-month waiting period, subject to sub-limits of ₹75,000 for normal delivery and ₹1,00,000 for caesarean delivery.

**Q7. Is pre-existing diabetes or hypertension covered?**
Yes, after a continuous coverage period of 36 months from policy inception, provided the condition was disclosed at proposal stage.

**Q8. What is a Day Care Procedure?**
A medical/surgical procedure that, due to technological advancement, can be completed in under 24 hours (e.g., cataract surgery, dialysis), and is covered without requiring an overnight hospital stay.

**Q9. Does this plan offer cashless treatment?**
Yes, cashless treatment is available at over 8,500 network hospitals across India.

**Q10. What happens if I go to a non-network hospital?**
You may still be treated and claim on a reimbursement basis by submitting original bills and reports post-treatment.

**Q11. Is there a co-payment on this policy?**
No co-payment applies for the primary insured family (self, spouse, children). A 10% co-payment applies only if dependent parents above age 65 are added under the optional extension.

**Q12. What is the No Claim Bonus?**
For every claim-free Policy Year, your Sum Insured increases by 10%, up to a maximum of 50%, at no extra premium cost.

**Q13. Does the policy cover AYUSH treatments?**
Yes, in-patient AYUSH treatments (Ayurveda, Yoga, Unani, Siddha, Homeopathy) at recognised AYUSH hospitals are covered up to the Sum Insured.

**Q14. Is a newborn baby automatically covered?**
Yes, from Day 1 of birth till the end of the Policy Year, subject to the sub-limits specified in the Policy Schedule; the child must be formally added at the next renewal for continued cover.

**Q15. How soon must I intimate a claim?**
Within 48 hours of emergency admission, or 48 hours prior to a planned/elective admission.

**Q16. Can I renew this policy for life?**
Yes, this policy offers lifelong renewability, provided premiums are paid on time and there is no fraud or misrepresentation.

**Q17. What if I miss my renewal date?**
You have a 30-day Grace Period to renew; however, there is no coverage for the Grace Period itself, and missing this window forfeits your accrued No Claim Bonus and waiting period credits.

**Q18. Does the policy cover air ambulance?**
Standard ambulance cover under this plan is for road ambulance only, up to ₹3,000 per hospitalisation event; air ambulance is not covered under the base plan.

**Q19. Will my claim be settled if I don't disclose a pre-existing illness?**
Non-disclosure of a known pre-existing condition can lead to claim repudiation or policy cancellation under the "Duty of Disclosure" principle.

**Q20. Is OPD (Out-Patient) treatment covered?**
No, this base plan covers in-patient hospitalisation, day care procedures, and defined pre/post-hospitalisation expenses; routine OPD consultations are not covered under the base plan.

**Q21. What documents are essential for a cancer-related claim?**
Histopathology/biopsy report, oncologist's treatment plan, hospital bills, and discharge summary are essential, in addition to the standard claim documents listed in Section 11.

**Q22. Can I switch (port) this policy from another insurer?**
Yes, subject to portability regulations issued by IRDAI, prior policy tenure and accrued benefits such as waiting periods already served may be carried forward, subject to underwriting.
