# 3. About the Policy

**ApexCare Elevate Health Plan (AC/EHP/2026)** is a premium, digital-first health insurance plan built for policyholders who want zero-compromise hospitalisation cover paired with a fully app-based servicing experience — from buying the policy to tracking a claim in real time.

The plan is designed around three pillars:

- **Zero deduction cover:** No capping on room rent, no proportionate deduction, and coverage for modern treatment methods (robotic surgery, oral chemotherapy, immunotherapy) that are often sub-limited elsewhere.
- **Digital servicing:** End-to-end claim tracking, instant e-Health Card issuance, and a dedicated Personal Health Coach accessible via the ApexCare App.
- **High-value protection:** Super top-up compatible base plan, unlimited restoration, and a dedicated Second Medical Opinion service for critical diagnoses.

This Policy Document, the Policy Schedule, the Customer Information Sheet, the Proposal Form, and any endorsements together form the complete contract between the Policyholder and ApexCare Health Insurance Pvt. Ltd.

# 8. Benefits

### 8.1 In-Patient Hospitalisation Cover
Covers room rent (no capping, zero proportionate deduction), nursing charges, ICU/ICCU charges, surgeon's and doctor's fees, anaesthesia, operation theatre charges, medicines, implants, and diagnostic tests for hospitalisation exceeding 24 hours.

### 8.2 Pre-Hospitalisation & Post-Hospitalisation Cover
- **Pre-Hospitalisation:** Medical expenses incurred up to 60 days prior to admission, related to the same illness/injury.
- **Post-Hospitalisation:** Medical expenses incurred up to 180 days after discharge, related to the same illness/injury.

### 8.3 Modern Treatment Cover
Covers advanced treatment methods including robotic surgery, oral chemotherapy, immunotherapy, stereotactic radiosurgery, intra-vitreal injections, and other listed modern treatments, up to the full Sum Insured (Elevate Prime), rather than the reduced sub-limits often applied to such treatments.

### 8.4 Restoration Benefit
Under Elevate Prime, the Sum Insured is automatically restored 100%, unlimited number of times per Policy Year, and is available even for the same illness (subject to a single restored claim per distinct illness episode). Under Elevate Core, restoration is available once per Policy Year for a subsequent unrelated illness/injury.

### 8.5 Personal Health Coach (Elevate Prime)
Insured Persons with a diagnosed chronic condition (e.g., diabetes, hypertension) are assigned a dedicated Personal Health Coach through the ApexCare App, providing periodic check-ins, diet and lifestyle guidance, and coordination with network specialists.

### 8.6 Second Medical Opinion
On diagnosis of any of 20+ listed critical illnesses, the Insured Person is entitled to a free second medical opinion from ApexCare's panel of specialists, to support informed treatment decisions.

### 8.7 Day Care Procedures
Covers all medical/surgical procedures not requiring 24-hour hospitalisation due to technological advancement, without restriction to a specific list, subject to medical necessity.

### 8.8 AYUSH Treatment Cover
In-patient AYUSH treatment (Ayurveda, Yoga & Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, Homeopathy) at a registered AYUSH hospital is covered up to the Sum Insured.

### 8.9 Annual Health Check-up
A comprehensive annual health check-up, including advanced diagnostic panels, is offered to all Insured Persons under Elevate Prime, regardless of claim history.

### 8.10 Optional Covers / Add-ons

| Add-on | Description |
|---|---|
| Critical Illness Booster | Additional lump sum benefit on first diagnosis of a listed critical illness |
| Super Top-Up Policy (Separate Product) | Provides additional cover above a specified threshold/deductible, linked to this base policy |
| Global Second Opinion | Extends the Second Medical Opinion service to an international panel of specialists |
| Maternity & New-born Cover | Covers maternity expenses and new-born baby cover after a specified waiting period |
| Air Ambulance Enhancement | Increases the standard air ambulance sub-limit |

# 10. Exclusions

### 10.1 Permanent Exclusions
- Cosmetic or plastic surgery, unless necessitated by an accident or medically required reconstruction.
- Treatment for obesity/weight control surgery, unless medically necessary and pre-authorised.
- Dental treatment, unless requiring hospitalisation due to accidental injury.
- Treatment for alcoholism, substance abuse, or any addictive condition.
- Expenses arising from self-inflicted injury, attempted suicide, or participation in an unlawful act.
- War, invasion, act of foreign enemy, hostilities, civil war, nuclear, biological, or chemical contamination.

- Non-allopathic treatment not undertaken at a registered AYUSH hospital.
- Hospitalisation primarily for diagnostic purposes, not followed by active treatment.
- Expenses incurred outside India, unless a specific international cover add-on has been opted for.

# 11. Claim Process

### 11.1 Cashless Claim Process (Network Hospital)

1. Locate an ApexCare network hospital via the App, website, or helpline.
2. Present the ApexCare e-Health Card (available in the App) and a valid photo ID at the hospital's insurance desk.
3. The hospital submits a Pre-Authorisation Request electronically to ApexCare.
4. ApexCare reviews and communicates its decision, typically within 1 hour of receipt of complete information, in line with IRDAI cashless claim turnaround guidelines. Claim status is visible in real time on the App.
5. On discharge, the hospital submits the final bill directly to ApexCare for settlement, subject to policy terms.

### 11.2 Reimbursement Claim Process (Non-Network Hospital)

1. Intimate ApexCare within 24 hours of emergency admission (or prior to admission for planned hospitalisation), via the App or helpline.
2. Settle hospital bills directly and retain all original bills, reports, and the discharge summary.
3. Upload the completed Claim Form and scanned original documents through the App within 15 days of discharge.
4. ApexCare's Claims Team reviews the submission and may request additional clarification or documents via the App.
5. On approval, the eligible claim amount is credited directly to the Policyholder's registered bank account.

### 11.3 Claim Settlement Timelines
- Cashless pre-authorisation decision: within 1 hour of receipt of complete information.
- Final cashless bill settlement: within 3 hours of receipt of complete discharge documents, wherever feasible.
- Reimbursement claims: settled within 15 days of receipt of all required documents, as per applicable regulatory timelines.

# 13. Premium Payment Rules

### 13.1 Grace Period
A Grace Period of **30 days** is allowed for renewal after the Policy expiry date. Continuity benefits (waiting periods served, moratorium period) are preserved if renewed within this period, though coverage remains suspended until renewal premium is paid.

### 13.2 Renewal
The Policy is renewable for life, subject to timely payment of premium. ApexCare shall not deny renewal on the sole ground of age or past claims, except on grounds of established fraud or moral hazard, in accordance with IRDAI guidelines.

### 13.3 Free Look Period
The Policyholder may review this Policy within **30 days** of receipt of the Policy Document and may cancel it if not agreeable to the terms, provided no claim has been made, subject to deduction of proportionate risk premium for the period on cover and any expenses already incurred by the Company.

### 13.4 Cancellation & Refund
- The Policyholder may cancel the Policy at any time with written notice, and shall be entitled to a refund of premium for the unexpired period on a short-period scale, provided no claim has been made during the Policy Year.
- ApexCare may cancel the Policy on grounds of established fraud or non-disclosure, subject to 15 days' prior written notice and applicable law.

### 13.5 Portability
Policyholders may port this Policy to another insurer, or migrate to another ApexCare product, at renewal, subject to IRDAI portability guidelines, carrying forward continuity benefits up to the Sum Insured of the previous policy.

# 15. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1. What makes ApexCare Elevate different from a standard health plan?**
It combines zero-deduction hospitalisation cover with a fully digital servicing layer — instant e-Health Card, real-time claim tracking, and a dedicated Personal Health Coach under the Prime variant.

**Q2. What is "zero proportionate deduction"?**
It means that even where an Insured Person chooses a higher room category, associated charges (doctor's fees, nursing, etc.) are not reduced proportionately, unlike plans that apply a proportionate deduction clause.

**Q3. Are robotic surgery and immunotherapy covered?**
Yes, under the Elevate Prime variant, modern treatment methods including robotic surgery, oral chemotherapy, and immunotherapy are covered up to the full Sum Insured.

**Q4. What is the Personal Health Coach benefit?**
Insured Persons with diagnosed chronic conditions are assigned a coach via the ApexCare App for ongoing lifestyle and condition management support.

**Q5. Can the Restoration Benefit be used for the same illness?**
Under Elevate Prime, yes — restoration is available even for the same illness, subject to one restored claim per distinct illness episode. Under Elevate Core, restoration applies to a subsequent unrelated illness.

**Q6. What is the waiting period for pre-existing diseases?**
36 months of continuous coverage from the date of first inception of the policy with ApexCare, as per applicable IRDAI norms.

**Q7. How fast is cashless approval?**
As per IRDAI norms, cashless pre-authorisation decisions are typically communicated within 1 hour of receipt of complete information from the hospital, and status can be tracked live on the App.

**Q8. Can I submit reimbursement claims fully online?**
Yes, the ApexCare App supports end-to-end digital submission of reimbursement claims, including document upload and status tracking.

**Q9. What is the moratorium period?**
After 5 years of continuous coverage, ApexCare cannot reject a claim on grounds of non-disclosure or misrepresentation, except where fraud is established.

**Q10. Is there a maximum entry age?**
No, there is no maximum entry age, in line with IRDAI's lifetime renewability guidelines.

**Q11. What happens if I miss my renewal date?**
A Grace Period of 30 days is provided. Coverage remains suspended during this period; continuity benefits are preserved only if renewed within the Grace Period.

**Q12. Can I port my existing policy to ApexCare?**
Yes, subject to IRDAI portability guidelines, at the time of renewal of your existing policy.

**Q13. Is a Super Top-Up policy included?**
No, it is a separate product that can be purchased alongside this base plan to extend cover above a chosen deductible.

**Q14. What is the Second Medical Opinion benefit?**
On diagnosis of a listed critical illness, Insured Persons can access a free second opinion from ApexCare's specialist panel to support treatment decisions.

**Q15. Are pre and post hospitalisation expenses covered?**
Yes, 60 days before admission and 180 days after discharge, for expenses related to the same illness/injury.

**Q16. Is maternity covered under the base plan?**
No, maternity is available only through an optional add-on, subject to a 36-month waiting period and applicable sub-limits.

**Q17. What is excluded under this Policy?**
Cosmetic treatments, dental treatment (unless due to accident), self-inflicted injury, and unproven/experimental treatments are among key exclusions. Refer to Section 10 for the full list.

**Q18. How can I track my claim status?**
Through the ApexCare App in real time, or by contacting the 24x7 toll-free helpline with your claim reference number.

**Q19. Is an annual health check-up included?**
Yes, under Elevate Prime, all Insured Persons are entitled to a comprehensive annual health check-up, irrespective of claims made in the previous Policy Year.

**Q20. What tax benefit is available on my premium?**
Premiums qualify for deduction under Section 80D of the Income Tax Act, 1961, subject to prescribed limits based on the age of the insured members.

**Q21. Can I enhance my Sum Insured at renewal?**
Yes, subject to underwriting approval; a fresh waiting period applies only to the incremental Sum Insured amount.

**Q22. Does ApexCare offer an e-Health Card?**
Yes, an e-Health Card is issued instantly through the ApexCare App upon policy issuance, and can be used at any network hospital.
