## 3. About the Policy

SecureLife Term Protect Plus is an **individual, non-linked, non-participating pure life risk protection plan** designed to provide a lump sum financial safeguard to the family of the Life Assured in the unfortunate event of death during the Policy Term. The plan is built for individuals who wish to secure their family's financial future — covering liabilities such as home loans, children's education, and daily living expenses — at an affordable premium.

This plan does **not** build cash value in the traditional sense (except where the optional Return of Premium variant is selected) and is designed purely to provide high-value life cover. SecureLife Term Protect Plus also offers built-in and optional benefits such as Terminal Illness Benefit, Accidental Death Benefit, Waiver of Premium on Disability, and a structured Income Benefit payout option, allowing the Policyholder to customise the plan to their family's specific needs.

This is a **health-and-life composite offering** of SecureLife Insurance Pvt. Ltd.'s Life & Protection Division, underwritten separately from the company's health indemnity products.

## 8. Benefits

### 8.1 Death Benefit

On the death of the Life Assured during the Policy Term, provided the policy is in force, the nominee/beneficiary shall receive the **"Sum Assured on Death,"** which is the highest of:

- 11 times the Annualised Premium; or
- The absolute Sum Assured stated in the Policy Schedule (₹75,00,000); or
- 105% of all premiums paid as on the date of death.

The Death Benefit is payable as a lump sum, or in a combination of lump sum and structured monthly income, as chosen by the Policyholder at inception or by the nominee at the time of claim (where the plan variant permits this flexibility).

### 8.2 Terminal Illness Benefit

If the Life Assured is diagnosed with a Terminal Illness (a condition expected, in the opinion of a Medical Practitioner, to lead to death within 6 months), SecureLife Insurance Pvt. Ltd. will advance up to **100% of the Sum Assured, subject to a maximum of ₹1,00,00,000,** ahead of death, provided the policy has been in force for at least 6 months and is not under any exclusion. This benefit is built into the base plan at no additional premium and terminates the base policy upon payment.

### 8.3 Accidental Death Benefit (Optional Rider)

Where opted for at inception, an additional lump sum equal to the Accidental Death Benefit Sum Assured is payable **over and above** the Death Benefit if death occurs directly and solely as a result of an accident, within 180 days of the accident, and the accident is the sole and direct cause of death independent of any other cause.

### 8.4 Waiver of Premium Rider (Optional)

If the Life Assured suffers an Accidental Total Permanent Disability during the Policy Term, all future premiums under the base policy and any attached riders are waived for the remainder of the Premium Paying Term, while the life cover continues uninterrupted.

### 8.5 Income Benefit (Optional Payout Structure)

The Policyholder may opt, at inception, for the Death Benefit to be paid partly as a lump sum (minimum 50%) and the balance as a **Monthly Income Benefit**, payable to the nominee for a fixed period of 10 years from the date of the claim, escalating at a simple rate of 5% per annum to help offset inflation.

### 8.6 Optional Riders Available

| Rider Name | Description | Additional Premium (Indicative) |
|---|---|---|
| Accidental Death Benefit Rider | Additional lump sum on accidental death | ₹1.20 per ₹1,000 Sum Assured p.a. |
| Waiver of Premium Rider | Waives future premiums on Accidental Total Permanent Disability | ₹0.55 per ₹1,000 Sum Assured p.a. |
| Critical Illness Rider | Lump sum on diagnosis of 15 specified critical illnesses | ₹2.10 per ₹1,000 Sum Assured p.a. |
| Return of Premium Option | Returns 100% of base premiums paid (excl. GST and rider premium) on survival to maturity | Increases base premium by approx. 40–60% |

## 9. Exclusions

### 9.1 Suicide Exclusion

If the Life Assured, whether sane or insane, dies by suicide within 12 months from the Risk Commencement Date, or from the date of revival of the policy, the nominee shall be entitled to at least 80% of the total premiums paid till the date of death, provided the policy is in force. No benefit beyond this shall be payable, and the policy shall terminate.

### 9.2 General Exclusions

No benefit is payable under this policy if death or disability arises directly or indirectly from:

- Self-inflicted injury, other than as covered under the suicide clause above.
- Death or disability arising from participation in a criminal or unlawful act.
- Death or disability caused by war, invasion, act of foreign enemy, hostilities (whether war is declared or not), civil war, rebellion, revolution, or participation in military, naval, or air force operations.
- Death arising from the influence of intoxicating substances, alcohol, or drugs not prescribed by a registered Medical Practitioner.
- Death or disability resulting from participation in hazardous sports or activities (e.g., mountaineering, scuba diving, aviation other than as a fare-paying passenger) not declared at proposal and not covered by an appropriate loading.
- Death caused by nuclear, chemical, or biological contamination, or radioactive contamination.
- Non-disclosure or misrepresentation of material facts at the time of proposal, as governed by Section 45 of the Insurance Act, 1938.

### 9.3 Accidental Death Benefit Rider – Additional Exclusions

- Death occurring after 180 days from the date of the accident.
- Death caused by a pre-existing physical or mental infirmity/disease.
- Death while under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
- Death arising from participation in adventure sports not declared to the Company.

## 10. Claim Process

### 10.1 Step-by-Step Death Claim Process

1. **Intimation:** The nominee/claimant intimates the claim to SecureLife Insurance Pvt. Ltd. via toll-free number, email, website claim portal, or nearest branch, as soon as possible after the death of the Life Assured.
2. **Claim Reference Generation:** A unique Claim Reference Number is issued within 24 working hours of intimation.
3. **Document Collection:** The claimant submits the duly filled claim form along with all required documents (see Section 11) to the nearest branch or via the online claim portal.
4. **Verification & Investigation:** The Company verifies the documents and, where required (e.g., early death claims within 3 years of policy issuance/revival, or unnatural death), conducts an investigation.
5. **Claim Assessment:** The claims team assesses the claim against policy terms, exclusions, and disclosures made at proposal stage.
6. **Decision Communication:** The Company communicates its decision (approval, part-approval, or repudiation with reasons) in writing within 30 days of receipt of all required documents, extendable to 90 days in cases requiring investigation, as per IRDAI claim settlement timelines.
7. **Payout:** On approval, the claim amount is disbursed via NEFT/RTGS directly to the nominee's registered bank account, typically within 7 working days of approval.

## 12. Nomination Rules

- Nomination shall be made in accordance with **Section 39 of the Insurance Act, 1938**, as amended from time to time.
- The Policyholder may nominate one or more persons to receive the policy benefits in the event of the Life Assured's death.
- Where multiple nominees are appointed, the Policyholder must specify the percentage share payable to each nominee; in the absence of such specification, the benefit shall be shared equally.
- The Policyholder may change or cancel a nomination at any time during the Policy Term prior to the maturity/claim, by submitting a written request along with the original policy document, in the form prescribed by the Company.
- Where the nominee is a minor at the time of nomination, the Policyholder must appoint an Appointee who shall be entitled to receive the benefit on behalf of the minor nominee until the nominee attains majority (18 years of age).
- A nomination once registered is valid until it is expressly cancelled or changed by the Policyholder by a written endorsement.
- Where the nominee predeceases the Policyholder/Life Assured, the benefit shall be payable to the legal heirs of the Life Assured, unless a fresh nomination has been recorded.
- In the case of a Married Women's Property Act (MWPA) policy, nomination provisions under Section 39 shall not apply, and payout shall be governed strictly by the beneficiaries named under the MWPA endorsement.

## 13. Premium Payment Rules

### 13.1 Grace Period

- **Annual/Half-Yearly/Quarterly mode:** 30 days from the premium due date.
- **Monthly mode:** 15 days from the premium due date.
- The life cover continues in full during the Grace Period. If the Life Assured dies during the Grace Period, the Death Benefit is payable after deduction of the outstanding premium (and any applicable rider premium) for the current policy year.

### 13.2 Lapse

- If the premium is not paid within the Grace Period, the policy **lapses**, and all benefits (including riders) cease immediately from the due date of the first unpaid premium.
- A lapsed policy has no surrender value under this plan (except where the Return of Premium option has been selected and the policy has acquired a paid-up/surrender value as per the applicable rules).

### 13.3 Revival

- A lapsed policy may be revived within **5 years** from the date of the first unpaid premium (the "Revival Period"), subject to:
 - Submission of a Revival Application Form and Declaration of Good Health.
 - Payment of all overdue premiums with interest, at the rate declared by the Company from time to time (currently 9.5% per annum, compounded).
 - Fresh medical underwriting, where required, based on the sum at risk and time elapsed since lapse.
 - The Company's approval of the revival application, at its sole discretion, based on underwriting assessment.
- If not revived within the Revival Period, the policy shall terminate, and no benefits shall be payable.

### 13.4 Surrender

- SecureLife Term Protect Plus, being a pure term protection plan, **does not acquire a Surrender Value** under the Regular Pay and Limited Pay (non-Return-of-Premium) variants, as no savings component exists.
- Under the **Return of Premium variant**, a Guaranteed Surrender Value becomes payable after the policy has acquired 2 full years' premiums, calculated as a percentage of total premiums paid (excluding taxes, rider premiums, and underwriting extra premiums), as per the Surrender Value table filed with IRDAI.

### 13.5 Free Look Period

- The Policyholder may review the terms and conditions of the policy within **30 days** from the date of receipt of the policy document (whether received physically or electronically).
- If the Policyholder disagrees with any terms and conditions, the policy may be returned for cancellation, stating the reasons for objection.
- On cancellation within the Free Look Period, the Company shall refund the premium paid, after deducting:
 - Proportionate risk premium for the period of cover.
 - Stamp duty charges.
 - Cost of medical examination, if any, undergone for the policy.

## 14. Tax Benefits

- Premiums paid towards SecureLife Term Protect Plus (including eligible riders) are generally eligible for deduction under **Section 80C of the Income Tax Act, 1961**, subject to the overall limit prescribed under that section and applicable only under the old tax regime.
- The Death Benefit and Terminal Illness Benefit received under this policy are generally exempt from tax under **Section 10(10D) of the Income Tax Act, 1961**, subject to the conditions specified therein, including the condition that the annual premium in any year does not exceed the prescribed percentage of the Sum Assured.
- Premiums paid towards the Critical Illness Rider (if opted) may be eligible for deduction under **Section 80D**, subject to applicable sub-limits.
- Tax laws are subject to change. Policyholders are advised to consult an independent tax advisor for guidance specific to their circumstances, as the Company does not offer tax advisory services.

## 15. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1. What is SecureLife Term Protect Plus?**
It is an individual, pure life risk protection plan that pays a lump sum (or lump sum plus income) to your nominee if you pass away during the Policy Term.

**Q2. Does this plan have any maturity benefit?**
Under the standard Regular Pay/Limited Pay variant, there is no maturity benefit. If you opt for the Return of Premium variant, all base premiums paid (excluding GST and rider premiums) are returned on survival to maturity.

**Q3. Can I increase my Sum Assured after the policy has started?**
Sum Assured enhancement is not available mid-term under this plan. You may purchase a fresh policy or an eligible top-up product, subject to underwriting.

**Q4. What happens if I stop paying premiums?**
Your policy enters the Grace Period, and if premiums remain unpaid thereafter, the policy lapses and cover ceases.

**Q5. Can I revive a lapsed policy?**
Yes, within 5 years from the date of the first unpaid premium, subject to payment of overdue premiums with interest and satisfactory medical underwriting.

**Q6. Is smoking status relevant to my premium?**
Yes. Premiums for tobacco users are higher than for non-tobacco users, reflecting differing mortality risk, and are determined based on declarations made at proposal stage.

**Q7. What is the Terminal Illness Benefit?**
If you are diagnosed with a condition expected to result in death within 6 months, you can claim up to 100% of your Sum Assured (max ₹1,00,00,000) in advance, and the policy terminates after this payout.

**Q8. Is COVID-19 or any pandemic-related death covered?**
Yes, death due to any illness, including pandemic-related illness, is covered under the base Death Benefit unless it falls under a specific exclusion listed in Section 9.

**Q9. Will my claim be rejected if I didn't disclose a pre-existing condition?**
Non-disclosure of material facts known at proposal stage can lead to claim repudiation or policy cancellation, subject to the provisions of Section 45 of the Insurance Act, 1938.

**Q10. Can I change my nominee after the policy is issued?**
Yes, you may change your nominee at any time during the Policy Term by submitting a written request in the prescribed format.

**Q11. What is the minimum and maximum entry age for this plan?**
Minimum entry age is 18 years, and maximum entry age is 65 years.

**Q12. Can I pay premiums monthly?**
Yes, monthly premium payment via ECS/auto-debit is available, with an applicable frequency loading of 4%.

**Q13. What happens if death occurs during the Grace Period?**
The Death Benefit remains payable, after deduction of the outstanding premium for the current policy year.

**Q14. Is the Accidental Death Benefit paid in addition to the base Death Benefit?**
Yes, if opted at inception, the Accidental Death Benefit is paid over and above the base Sum Assured, provided death occurs within 180 days of the accident and meets policy conditions.

**Q15. Can I port this policy to another insurer?**
Term life policies are typically not "ported" in the way health policies are; however, you may surrender/discontinue this policy and apply afresh with another insurer, subject to fresh underwriting by that insurer.

**Q16. Is there a medical test required before I can buy this plan?**
Medical tests may be required depending on your age, declared Sum Assured, health disclosures, and other underwriting factors, as determined by the Company.

**Q17. What is the Free Look Period?**
It is a 30-day window from receipt of your policy document during which you may review the policy and cancel it if you disagree with its terms, subject to specified deductions.

**Q18. Can I get a joint life policy under this plan?**
SecureLife Term Protect Plus is designed as an individual, single-life policy and does not offer a joint-life variant.

**Q19. How is the claim amount paid out — lump sum or monthly?**
You may choose at inception (or your nominee may choose at claim stage, where the variant permits) between a full lump sum, or a combination of lump sum plus a 10-year Monthly Income Benefit.

**Q20. What documents does my family need to keep ready in case of a claim?**
The original policy document, a valid ID proof of the nominee, the Death Certificate, and bank account details are the essential documents; a complete list is provided in Section 11.

**Q21. Does the policy cover death outside India?**
Yes, death occurring anywhere in the world is covered, subject to submission of documents equivalent to those specified in Section 11, duly apostilled/attested as required, and subject to standard policy exclusions.

**Q22. What is Waiver of Premium Rider and how does it help?**
If you suffer an Accidental Total Permanent Disability, this rider (if opted) waives all future premiums while your life cover continues uninterrupted.
